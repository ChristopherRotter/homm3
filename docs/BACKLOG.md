## Ordering rationale

*(2026-10-31, TWN-Q1)* E09 (towns) opens now in a new **town** lane, in parallel with the adventure lane's E08 (its increment 9, then 10 to 12 as sketched), rather than after E08: 128 of the 160 shipped maps wait only for a hero generated at a main town. The town lane owns `h3-towns` and the town modules inside the adventure lane's crates; the crates' common parts, versions and replays stay the adventure lane's and are changed `cross-lane`, merged in order.

*(2026-10-27, E06 T8c)* E06's targets were revised at increment 7's checkpoint (the user): six levers left PPO at the heuristic bots' level, and a decision-time search gained nothing over its network. E06 closes with `t7-1` as the battle AI v1; the stronger agent is E25's. *(2026-10-27, E06 T9b)* The review found the search's measurement flawed (a terminal scored ±1 against the value head's reward v1; escapes below the root, fixed): its verdict is inconclusive, and E25 measures again. E06 closed the same day (T9d).

*(2026-10-24, Q58)* E11 is re-scoped from "AB and SoD completeness" (folded: every epic covers the three editions for what it builds) to the historical rules of RoE and AB. The order (the user, 2026-10-24): the battle lane closes E23 first (done the same night), spends one session on its inbox's leftovers, and only then opens E11 with T1, once the user's original media are at hand; until then E11 waits without blocking anything.

*(2026-10-23, CLT-Q18)* The client lane split at E17 M3's closure: the client lane continues E17 as the battle client (M3's last task, then sieges as M4 and live play as M5), and the new map-client lane opens with E24, the adventure map client, in parallel on the same foundation (the client foundation and the capture harness are common paths).

*(2026-10-18, AI-Q1)* The AI lane opened with E05 (`h3-sim`, `h3-ffi`, `python/`). Order: **E05, then E23, then E06**: training uses only self-play and the system actor, and the system actor of neutral sides is E23's policy, so the neutral share of training waits for it (until then the random policy holds the seat, for tests only). *(2026-10-20, AI-Q8)* E06 starts beside E23: its training on the kinds a player plays on both sides needs no system actor; only its neutral share waits for E23's policy of a side without a hero.

*(2026-10-07, Phase 0)* Work runs in lanes (`docs/08-work-process.md`, "Lanes"); the Lane column says which lane owns an epic. **Adventure (E07–E10) moved ahead of E05/E06**: the battle lane continues E04b and E04c while the adventure lane starts with E07/E08 in parallel; E05/E06 (simulation env, battle AI) follow in the battle lane until an AI lane opens. The server (E16) and client (E17) lanes refine and write their ADRs now and implement once the battle actions are mostly stable (E04b); the client starts with the user's asset notes (E17a). This table is a shared path: its status cells change when an epic starts or ends; the lane HANDOFFs carry the detail.

*(2026-10-01)* The former E04 is now E04a, E04b and E04c, in that order: magic builds on the hero seam, and the special battlefields' school-based effects build on magic. Comments in code and older docs that say "E04" mean this group; the tasks they refer to are listed in the epic files and in HANDOFF.

1. **E01 first** validates determinism, the command/event model, security shape, performance, gates and the sim API on the smallest possible slice.
2. **E02–E03 before AI**: the battle must be faithful before it is worth training on.
3. **E05–E06 early**: the AI is the motivator; a working battle training loop de-risks the performance and API decisions sooner than a large adventure map would.
4. Adventure/town/game (E07–E10) follow; security projections (E10) build on the skeleton's observation API.
5. Map generator, long-tail objects, clients and server come when their dependencies exist.
