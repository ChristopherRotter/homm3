# Adventure lane — what exists

The code map of the adventure lane, moved out of its HANDOFF's "Current state" on 2026-10-24 (verbatim; the HANDOFF keeps the position, the next step, the versions and the ids). Read the entry for the area your task touches: crates and modules by task (`T<n>`), the rule ids they carry (`ADV-*`, `GAM-*`, `HER-*`), and the versions they bump. Add a line here when a task builds a new area; the task's full record goes to `docs/epics/E08-log.md`.

- **Map reader and import** (`h3-map`): `read`/`parse` into `model::RawMap`; `import` into `h3_game::GameSetup`, `import_map` its static layer (`MAP_IMPORT_VERSION` 11, `docs/formats/h3m.md` "Import"; since T54 the players' human flags, the objects' scenery, a placed hero's sex). Every shipped map's static layer imports and validates; of the 160 shipped maps 5 import whole (9 heroes, 41 levels at load), the rest are typed `Unsupported`. `verify --with-original-data` runs `crates/h3-map/examples/original_data.rs` on the shipped maps.
- **Monsters, grounds, guarded piles** (T20a2a to T21a; ADV-MON-002 to -010, ADV-GRD-001, ADV-PIK-004): kinds `Monster`, `Ground`; `[params.monsters]`; `ObjectSetup.monster`/`.drawn`; `army_left`; `battle_ground`; `guard_of`; `answer.rs`; `split` (flag 24); `Defender::{Monster, Guards}`, `Actor::System`, `end_monster_battle`; `MonsterChanged`; `state/piles.rs`.
- **The store of heroes** (T18b, GAM-HER-001): `GameState` keeps `Arc<HeroDetails>` by `HeroId` (`GameHeroSetup.details` or defaults, validated by `h3-heroes`); `attack_defense`, `join_bonus`; `GameObservation.heroes`. T39: `movement.rs` (HER-ADV-003 to -007). T49a: `worn` by position (`positions.rs`, `PlaceArtifact`; re-exported by `h3_game`). T49b: `moves.rs`, `combine.rs`, `equip` (Titan's book), `meeting_spells`. Progression (T26b–T30): `level.rs`, `level_up.rs`, `GainLevel`, `offered`; `original_choice` (HER-LVL-006); `state/levels.rs` (GAM-HER-002, flag 27), levels at load (GAM-SET-002), in play (`give_levels`, `Decision::LevelUp`; GAM-DEC-002, GAM-BAT-008). T60a: `BattleBonuses` (`HeroDetails::bonuses`, HER-ADV-008, folded into `derive_battle_hero`'s morale and luck), `GainPrimary`, `RaiseSkill`, `Bonus`, `ClearBonuses` (`change.rs`). T60b: `reward.rs` (`grant`, `HeroReward`, `Granted`, `can_receive_artifact` under flag 38; HER-CHG-003), `teach.rs` (`hut_teaches`, `university_slot`, `scholar_reward`, `library_admits`, `learning_experience`, `next_level_experience`; with `spells.rs`' `can_learn_spell`, HER-CHG-004).
- **Objects** (T12–T15): types (`ruleset/objects.rs`, ADV-OBJ-002); contents drawn at the build (ADV-PIK-001 to -003); removals (`state/occupancy.rs`); `ObjectView`s (no index). Visits (ADV-OBJ-003, -004; `visit_at`, `tile_object`); gains and offers in `AdventureOutcome` (ADV-OBJ-005). Purchases and keys (`state/flags.rs`; ADV-VIS-001 to -004); `offer_stands`.
- **Battles** (T19a1, GAM-BAT-001 to -005; `h3_game::state::battle`): a step toward another player's hero visits it (`BattleStart`, `battle_ground`), `Decision::Battle(Arc<OpenBattle>)` both owners play (`GameCommand::Battle`), seeds under `ADV-QUIRK-5`, events and `GameObservation.battle` to the owners; the outcome's armies, `HeroRemoved`, a surrender's gold; the heroes' part (`heroes_after_battle`). Since T54 (GAM-BAT-010 to -013) the setup's difficulty, human sides (`PlayerSetup.human`), colours, heroes' sexes, the background (`AdventureState::background`, `Scenery`) and the attacker's ground (`special_ground`, `battle_at`); the retreat facts at their default (`ADV-STUB-9`).
- **Setups and state:** `h3_adventure::{AdventureSetup, AdventureState}` (static map behind an `Arc`, heroes by id with points, formation and tactics flag, the difficulty, state hash v11; ADV-MAP-001); T40: `start_game` after the levels at load, `start_turn`, `start_day` (`ADV-QUIRK-18`) read the store; `h3_game::{GameRuleset, GameSetup, GameState}`: a stream per player, sequential turn groups, treasuries, pending decisions (GAM-SET-001, GAM-TRN-*, GAM-CAL-001, GAM-RES-*, GAM-DEC-001). Game state hash v12 (T49a); `GameSetup::validate` builds with seed 0.
- **Movement** (T7b): `Pricing` in `movement.rs` (ADV-MOV-003 to -006, `ADV-QUIRK-2`/`-3`; T40 Pathfinding, walkers, `no_native`; T41 fly and water-walk prices, `ADV-QUIRK-17`), `native_terrain` (ADV-MOV-004, `ADV-QUIRK-4`), `MoveHero` (ADV-MOV-007, rejected whole, `HeroStepped` per step), `reach` (one path per tile); T41: a flyer's or water walker's passage (`state/passage.rs`, `NoStop`, ADV-MOV-009, -010, `ADV-QUIRK-15`) and pass nodes; no step down onto a hero on a tile closed above (T33).
- **Fog of war** (T8a, `BitGrid`, 32 × 32 leaves): an explored layer per player, `fog::reveal` (ADV-FOG-002, -003), moves and `Reach` on explored tiles only (ADV-MOV-008), events `{ kind, seen_by: Viewers }` (ADV-FOG-004); the fringe (ADV-FOG-005 under `ADV-QUIRK-1`), `observe` and `project` in both crates (ADV-FOG-006).
- **Commands** (T8b2): `h3_game::legal_commands` and `legal_moves` (a hero's `Reach`, the user), empty outside the turn.
- **The env** (T9a; the AI lane's since T45 but `replay.rs`): `AdventureEnv`, action encoding v4 (`EndTurn`, eight hero slots, the answers, a tile; in a battle the battle's; `to_move` yields `Seat`s, `Seat::Neutral` played as `Actor::System`), `random_rollout`.
- **Replays and benchmarks** (T9b): `adventure::replay` (format v6), `h3 adventure run/verify/bench`, ten golden replays of random play (options in `adventure_replays.rs`) and since T52 two scripted ones (`run --script`, `Replay::record_commands`: a meeting with Scholar, an army dialog), the maps `fixtures/maps/e08-{duel-36,xl-144,army-12}.h3m` (`h3-map`'s `tests/fixtures.rs`; T31: heroes near a level; T42: flyers, water walkers, the skills; T52: the army map, a mate and a full army), six gated workloads in `benches/adventure/` (play, reach by walkers and by lifted heroes, fork with a battle open, and since T52 with a meeting open, the management listing in a meeting).
- **Experiments**: `h3-experiment` (battle's) has the adventure shape; batches 1 to E6 scored (`e4_score`); agents run them on the harness (decision 21).
- **Army management** (T50): `army.rs` (`ArmyCommand`, `split_range`), `state/manage.rs` (the group passed in, `army_commands`); `max_stack_count` (ADV-ARM-007); `AdventureOutcome::{meeting, no_room}`. T51a: `h3_game::{Manage, legal_management, split_range}` (per hero), `deny_tactics` (GAM-BAT-009). T51b: `Decision::{Meeting, ArmyDialog}` (GAM-DEC-003, -004; Scholar at the opening), `GameState::{meeting, army_dialog}`, `Together` and `check_managing` in `h3-game`'s `state/manage.rs` (two armies only in either), the join offered whatever room (game state hash v13).
- **Adventure ruleset part** (v11 since T50: a stack's most): terrains, roads, rivers, movement and setup parameters, object types, starting resources by difficulty, battle terrains, monsters' parameters (with the price resource).
- **Properties** on generated playable maps (`h3-map`'s `play.rs`, `view.rs`): `Step::Fight` (hero casts via `h3-battle`, a dev-dependency), monsters, grounds, the system actor, piles, level-ups, `check_progression` per command; 192 fixed battle cases; T42: kits, flyers, water walkers, Nomads; T52: `Exchange` (a mate with Scholar, spells and Elixir's parts; a full army beside goblins), `Step::{Manage, Meet, Join}` and arbitrary management, `check_invariants`, `check_managing`, `check_management`, `check_groups`, `check_inverse`; 96 fixed management cases.
- **Ownership:** the four crates, `h3-heroes`, `h3-sim`'s `src/adventure/replay.rs`, `h3-cli`'s `src/adventure/` (`h3-cli`'s `Cargo.toml`, `lib.rs`, `main.rs` battle's, `h3-sim`'s the AI lane's: `cross-lane`), `crates/h3-cli/tests/adventure_replays.rs`, `fixtures/replays/adventure/`, `fixtures/maps/`, `benches/adventure/`, each pack's `adventure.toml`, `docs/formats/h3m.md`, `docs/rules/catalog/{adventure,game,heroes,town}.toml`.
