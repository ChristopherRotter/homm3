- *(2026-10-12, SRV-Q20, after E16 M4 T3b)* **Eager disconnect detection.** The server noticed a dead connection only by its idle timeout (5 min): a seat whose connection died silently stayed held that long before its grace began, and a server-side outage could end as a forfeit for the player whose total ran out before the other's drop was noticed, a rated loss once T3b rated results (against ADR-0019 point 6). Options: (a) server-side WebSocket pings with a short deadline; (b) in addition, the clocks standing still on a mass drop (a heuristic); (c) as built. **User: (a), now** (after T3b merged). Built (*provisional*, M4's review list): `[limits] ping_every_ms` (15 s; 0: none) and `ping_timeout_ms` (30 s, longer than the period): a connection the server heard nothing from, not even a pong, for the timeout closes (`Goodbye { IdleTimeout }`, bounded by a moment), the time the server spends writing to it not counted. A server-side outage now drops every seat within a ping of each other, so it ends void, not as a forfeit; (b) is not built (a forfeit can still land in that window of up to `ping_every_ms`).
- *(2026-10-12, SRV-Q19, E16 M4 refinement)* **Ranked battles: stalling, disconnects, seasons, one game per ladder.** (Stalling) A connected player who never moves stalls a ranked battle: the idle timeout drops silent connections only. - *(2026-10-11, SRV-Q13, after E16 M2 T5a; for M4)* **Ranked games: one at a time, a pause only by agreement, the paused game's deadline.** No code until M4 (ladders) and M3 (accounts: before them a player is a seat token per game); M2's unranked games stay as built. **User's rules:** (1) a player whose ranked game is not over starts no other ranked game; (2) a ranked game pauses only when both players agree (one proposes, the other accepts; a decline or no answer and the game goes on). The agent's reading of (1), to confirm at M4's refinement: on this server (servers do not synchronize), across its ladders, running and paused games alike, unranked games unaffected, the server's bot accounts exempt. The weighing: at the deadline (i) void, the cheap escape for a losing player who asked for a pause and never returns, while (1) keeps the opponent off the ladder; (ii) the absent player forfeits (the game is due, the disconnect grace runs; both absent: void, a no-show each); (iii) adjudication by the position, rejected (an evaluation players would dispute); (iv) a draw, half a point for the no-show. The length: (a) fixed per ladder (the user's 36 h): simple, but the due time is not chosen and a no-show makes the honest player wait it out; (b) negotiated within the ladder's bounds: both agree to the lock-out and to a due time, a short break has a short deadline; costs a field in `Pause`, an accept/decline exchange and a client picker; (c) a pause budget per player per game, best as (b)'s bound; (d) no long pause on battle ladders. The server's own downtime never counts against a player; T5a's `empty_game_ms` does not apply to ranked games. **User: (ii) with (b)**, the agent's recommendation: the absent player forfeits; the proposer names the pause's length within the ladder's bounds (the longest pause and the pauses per player and game, 0 for none: minutes on a battle ladder, up to 36 h on a whole-game ladder); the agreed due time journaled with the pause. Ladder ADR draft point 8 and `E16-design.md` §11 amended.
