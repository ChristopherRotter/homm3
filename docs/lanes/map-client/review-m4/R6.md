### R6-1 [bug] A shipped situation's `bonus = "gold"` is accepted but never applied
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:289-292` (`started`), with `:584-586` (`stage`) and `:333-334`
(`SHIPPED_KEYS`). What: `stage` computes `gold` from `bonus`/`players` and passes it to `started`,
but `started` hands it only to `h3drive.to_scenario`; the shipped branch calls `to_shipped(game)`,
which takes no `gold` and never opens the advanced options (`h3map.py:240-273`). So
`start-jared` and `start-good-witch` (`bonus = "gold"`, `players = 3`/`2`) start with the Random
bonus. That is the artifact draw T3b1 added `bonus` to avoid (`h3drive.gold_bonus`'s docstring: an
artifact "changes its luck, morale or primary skills for one run"). The same happens to a writer's
map with an underground (`shipped=... or "underground" in sit`); a seed there raises instead.
Evidence: the runbook (README 130-135) says "a shipped map's situation may give `bonus = "gold"`
with `players`", and so does the review list's T3b2 comparisons item. `kept/start-jared.toml`'s
`resources = [20, 10, 20, 10, 10, 10, 2000]` holds no gold bonus. `set.toml`'s `start-jared`
expects no `kingdom.resources` difference, while `engine-start-owners` (a writer's map, bonus
applied) expects one. Both shipped entries expect "`status.skills` differs", which is put down to
the level-up draws but could just as well be an artifact bonus. `h3map.py engine` and a re-capture
can change from run to run.
Suggested fix: give `to_shipped` the `gold` argument and call `h3drive.gold_bonus(game, gold)` on
the scenario list before Begin, as `to_scenario` does. Then capture both shipped starts again
(`set --only`) and re-record their `engine` entries. Or, the smallest fix: refuse `bonus` in
`check_situation` for a shipped or underground map. Effort: S.

- the sounds `[map.turn].week`/`month`, `finds.pickups`, `killfade`, `[map.level_up].sound` and
  `[map.cast].summon_boat`;
- the `AITheme0`-`2` tracks;
- the layout's `status_other`, `status_found` and `level_up` dialogs.

`AdventureLayout::names()` and `Tables::names()` exist but only their unit tests call them.

### R6-3 [risk] The battle step carries the palette's step only; the mouse poll and the status window's timed end keep the battle's real-time phase
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:1550-1566` (`sequence`, the battle step) against `:1400`
(`mem.align_mouse_poll(t0)`) and `h3mem.py:650-659`. What: the step writes back only
`PALETTE_TICK_TIME`. Two other deadlines were aligned at the sequence's 0, and after the battle
they hold a clock time set while the clock ran in real time:
- `MOUSE_POLL_NEXT` 0x69CA18, the 33-ms grid that the timeline's `cursor` point follows ("on the
  33-ms grid from 0"), with `SPELL_CURSOR_NEXT`;
- a timed status window's end (manager +0x39C: kind 6 for 5 000 ms, the date for 3 000).

So after a battle the cursor's point lines can land up to 32 ms apart from run to run, and so can
a timed window's end. The viewer, whose battle takes no time, keeps its grid from 0. The three
recorded battles move no pointer after the battle and ask no timed kind across one, so nothing
recorded shows this (unsure whether any planned sequence would). Design §5.19 names the palette
and the objects' deadline only.
Suggested fix: carry both like the palette, with `poll_ahead = u32(MOUSE_POLL_NEXT) - opened`
(and the spell cursor's), and `+0x39C` when a timed kind shows. Or list them in the step's
docstring and §5.19 as "not carried", and refuse an input after a battle that moves the pointer.
Effort: S.

### R6-4 [risk] A situation's `pointer` is undone in a sequence, yet the battle step restores it
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:1343` and `:1368-1371` (`sequence`), `:754-784`
(`look_over_view`), `:634-644` (`stage`). What: `stage` moves the mouse to the still's `pointer`
last. `sequence` then always calls `look_over_view`, which ends with
`game.move(*h3adv.PARK)`, so the sequence's 0 has the pointer parked. But `pointer` (the value
put back after a battle) starts from `sit["pointer"]`. A sequence on such a situation records a
parked pointer at 0, then after a battle puts the mouse where it never was. No sequence's
situation has a `pointer` today, and `check_situation` does not refuse one. The README's battle
section says "`h3adv.PARK` or its last `pointer`/`click`", which is the input, not the situation.
Suggested fix: refuse `pointer` in a sequence's situation (`sequence_inputs` or `stage(...,
sequence=True)`), or start `pointer` from `h3adv.PARK`. Effort: S.

### R6-5 [risk] A hero's `secondary` erases its `diplomacy`
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:392-397` (`lists`), `h3mem.py:1429-1447`. What:
`write_diplomacy` writes the level byte +0xCD alone. `write_secondary`, called after it, clears
all 28 levels (+0xC9) and slots before it writes the list. A hero given both loses its Diplomacy
unless the list repeats it. Neither `situation.rs` (1295-1314) nor the harness refuses the pair. No
situation has both today (`seq-ask-join-gold` has `diplomacy`; the level-up and cast situations
have `secondary`).
Suggested fix: write `secondary` first, or refuse both on one hero in `situation.rs`'s check (next
to "Diplomacy past Expert"). Effort: S.

### R6-6 [risk] The game's disk check: no pre-flight, and the trap's rule leaves out the floor
Where: `docs/lanes/map-client/TRAPS.md:6`; `clients/capture/h3drive.py` (`Game.start`, no check);
`docs/exe/map.toml` `main_menu_proc` 0x4FB710 and `free_disk_space` 0x50C7A0. What: the ballast
rule is manual. When it is wrong, every run fails with the misleading "`main_menu` did not
appear", and `started`'s retry cannot tell why. The trap says to keep the free bytes modulo 4 GiB
"below 2 GiB". But `main_menu_proc` also refuses below 0x500000 (5 MB: GENRLTXT 708, the game
ends), so the safe band is 5 MB to 2 GiB. The signed 32-bit reading is an observation (map.toml
has `free_disk_space` as "inferred … not read").
Suggested fix: in `Game.start`, check `os.statvfs` of the prefix's drive. If `f_bavail * f_frsize
% 2**32` lies outside [5 MiB, 2 GiB), raise "the game's disk check would fail: free bytes …
(TRAPS)". Correct the trap's band. Effort: S.

### R6-7 [risk] `frames` writes a `-live.toml` into the committed `timelines/` for a scratch or sound run

### R6-8 [risk, unsure] The options a sequence writes into memory can reach the prefix's registry
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:1355-1362` (the `OPTIONS` written with `mem.write_u32`);
`h3drive.py:477-503` (`stop`: only Army Info and the shades are put back); `docs/exe/map.toml`

Suggested fix: a cheap guard in `Game.stop`: read the five values' registry entries
(`setting_dword`) and fail if they changed, or put them back as for the shades. Effort: S.

### R6-11 [doc] `h3lod.py` names the wrong guard, and its `KEPT` differs from the guard's
Where: `clients/capture/h3lod.py:195-196, 205`; `clients/crates/h3-map-view/tests/texts.rs:12`.
What: the comment says "the client test `timeline::tests::texts` checks the kept files". There is
no such test: the guard is `tests/texts.rs`, as the README says. Python's `KEPT` uses `\s`
(Unicode white space: `\xa0`, `\x0b`, `\x0c` …), while the test's `KEPT` is `" \t\r\n"`. A
cp1252 no-break space in an original text would be kept by `label` and then fail the test. That
failure is loud, not a leak, but it is the wrong place to learn of it.
Suggested fix: fix the name. Make `KEPT` `[ \t\r\n\d+\-,.:;()%/'"!?]` with `\d` as `[0-9]`, the
test's set, so that both sides agree. Effort: S.

### R6-12 [risk] Missing engine binaries abort the whole `drawn` or `engine` run, and stale ones go unnoticed
Where: `clients/capture/h3map.py:1978-1996` (`engine_game`), `:1943-1947` (`drawn_sequences`
catches `RuntimeError` only), `:2025-2066` (`run_engine`: no catch); also `run_engine`'s
`kept.read_text()` (2043) and `run(...)` (2055). What: `subprocess.run` with a missing
`target/debug/h3` raises `FileNotFoundError`, not the `RuntimeError` that `drawn` treats per
sequence. So one missing build stops `drawn` for every sequence and situation after it, and
`run_engine` stops at its first entry. Nothing checks that the binaries were built from the
current tree (TRAPS 168-172: "build them first … and again after merging `master`"). A stale engine
shows as "a gap CHANGED", pointing at the viewer.
Suggested fix: check that `h3` and `h3-server` exist up front, with one clear message. Catch
`OSError` beside `RuntimeError` per sequence and per entry. Optionally compare the binaries'
mtime with the newest `crates/**.rs` and warn. Effort: S.
