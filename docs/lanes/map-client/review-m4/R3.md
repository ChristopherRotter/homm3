## Findings

### R3-1 [bug] A record's flag is raised in the colour of the owner's player id, not its colour
Where: `clients/crates/h3-map-view/src/engine/visits.rs:181` and `:195` (`told`: `ObjectFlagged {
owner, .. } => Some(*owner)`), used as a colour by `src/bin/h3-map-view/paint.rs` `record_finds`
(`flag: flag.map(|colour| (o, colour))`, three places) and then `Loop::raise` into `Owners`, which
the scene draws as a colour (`scene.rs:356`, `tables.map.owners.entry(c, owner)`).
What: the protocol's `ObjectFlagged.owner` is a `PlayerId` (`crates/h3-protocol/src/map/game/
event.rs:585-592`: `owner: owner.raw()`; `h3-adventure/src/command.rs:1452`: `owner: PlayerId`),
an index into the game's players, each with its own colour (`GameObservation::players`, "by id";
`Player::colour`). The rest of the viewer converts: the start's owners go through
`observation.players[p].colour` (`src/engine.rs:273-279`, `:332`), another player's turn too
(`played.rs:407-411`). The flags alone take the id as the colour. Every M4 game has Red = player 0
and Blue = player 1, so ids and colours agree and `game-flags` is exact; on a map whose players
skip a colour (Red and Tan: ids 0 and 1, colours 0 and 3) a flag raised by Tan shows Blue's, on the
view and the minimap, for the rest of the record. The design says the same thing wrong (§5.15:
"`ObjectFlagged { object_type, anchor, owner }` (the visitor's colour)").
Evidence: `tests/engine.rs:2141` builds the event with `owner: 2` and expects the find's flag
`Some(2)`, so the test pins the raw id and cannot catch this.
Suggested fix: convert in `played` when the visit's events are folded (the observation is at
hand there), or pass the players' colours to `visits::told`; add a test with ids and colours
apart; correct §5.15. Effort: S.

### R3-2 [bug] A neutral mine's guards beaten or not: the "beaten" box, the mine's box and the hero kept
Where: `src/engine/visits.rs:168-191` (`Told::Guarded`: `winner` stored, the hero's
`HeroRemoved` not looked at); `src/bin/h3-map-view/paint.rs:1226-1278` (the answer always
`after: AfterBattle::Boxes` with ADVEVENT 85's and MINEEVNT's boxes); `src/timeline.rs:4354-4360`
(`after_battle`: `(AfterBattle::Boxes, _)` opens the answer's boxes whatever the winner).
What: a record where the viewer's hero loses to, or retreats from, a mine's guards plays as a
win: the guards-beaten box and the mine's box with its income, then the visit's end, the hero
still on the map and selected. No flag is raised (the record has no `ObjectFlagged`), so the
mine's flag stays right, but the boxes and the hero are wrong for the rest of the record. The
monster's fight next to it (`Told::Fight`) does look at `HeroRemoved` (`removed(true)` →
`AfterBattle::Lost`). Not on T5b2's review item (its mine recording is a sure win).
Suggested fix: `Guarded` with the hero removed (or the defender the winner) takes `Lost` as a
monster's fight does and builds no boxes; a test with a lost guards' battle. Overlaps the battle
reviewer's area (the hand-over); the map's part (the boxes, the flag) is this one. Effort: S.

### R3-3 [bug] A boat first seen after a record's start is never drawn, and a boarding onto it refuses the record
Where: `src/engine.rs:369-381` (the start's boats are `observation.adventure.boats` of the first
observation only); `src/engine/played.rs:453` (`GameReveal` only folded into the observation:
`Played` carries no boats); `src/timeline.rs:5644-5651` (`boarded`: no empty boat on the tile →
`self.off(next)`, `Unplayable::Moved`); also `sea_unseen` (`:3282-3297`) for a computer's
boarding, through the same `boarded`.
What: the protocol's observation holds "the empty boats it sees" (`wire/game.rs:148`) and each
`AdventureReveal` "the empty boats it sees now ... where it did not" (`:430`). The viewer's
`Boats` is the start's and changes only by its own boardings, landings and casts. A boat outside
the start's sight (any boat the hero discovers by walking) is therefore never drawn (the binary
draws `live.boats` from `Draw::boats`), and when the viewer's hero (or a computer in sight) boards
it the step is "off the record" and the whole record stops. `game-boats` and the sea golden
replays had their boat in sight at 0, so nothing showed it. Not in the design or the review list
(only a Shipyard's built boat is named, §5.17: `economy-shipyard-s1`).
Suggested fix: fold each reveal's boats (and `BoatBuilt`, `BoatRemoved`) into the boats the
timeline holds, by walk or by turn table as the walks' revealed tiles are (`Move::revealed`);
until then an `MCL-Q`/stub and a refusal that names the cause instead of `Moved`. Effort: M.

### R3-4 [risk] Later and other flags of a record are dropped: the owner stays wrong for the rest of the game
Where: (a) `src/engine/visits.rs:293-305` (`hired`: none without `CreaturesJoined`, `JoinRefused`
or `NoneToRecruit` after an accept), so `told` gives nothing for a dwelling whose hire is declined
or whose creature is not free (the engine opens the hire dialog, `HireOpened`, and the purchase
is a later command), and its `ObjectFlagged` is never raised; (b) `paint.rs:1294` (a mine's or a
Lighthouse's second `Visited::Find` only sets `earlier.again = boxed` and `continue`s, its flag
lost) and `timeline.rs:3786-3792` (`tell_find` opens `again` as `Told::Plain`: no `raise`, no
minimap draw), so a mine the viewer takes back later in the game shows its box but keeps the
other player's flag; (c) `played.rs:508-559` (`other_event` folds steps, boats and removals, not
`ObjectFlagged`), so a computer taking a mine or a dwelling in sight leaves the old flag.
What: each is a wrong state carried to the record's end (view and minimap). The review list
names "a declined hire not built" (T4b2) but not that its flag is lost with it; (b) and (c) are
not named. In a whole game (the 8 shipped maps, the golden replays) all three are likely.
Suggested fix: raise the record's flag whatever box the viewer builds (a find with no boxes and
a flag; on a later visit the same), fold another player's `ObjectFlagged` into its turn as its
removals are; list what stays unbuilt as gaps. Effort: M.

### R3-5 [risk] Summon Boat's dissolves do not keep the status window their view draw chose (unsure)
Where: `src/timeline.rs:4590-4596` (`summon`) and `:4619-4623` (`arrive`): `before =
drawn.clone(); self.draw(t)`, against `lose` (`:4426-4441`), which T4b2 fixed to copy the status
window the draw chose into `before`.
What: `draw` runs `choose(t, false)`, which steps the date's window when it shows (design §4.9);
the dissolve then shows the old window outside its box for its 264 ms, the same class of
difference T4b2 found for the landing (`game-boats` 4250 to 4705, 20 790 pixels). The recordings
cast with the hero's window, which an unforced draw leaves as it is, so nothing showed. Unsure:
whether `summon_boat_cast`'s view draw goes through `status_window_choose` was not read (§5.8
says "the view drawn without it").
Suggested fix: one helper for "the screen before a view draw" used by `lose`, `summon` and
`arrive` (and noted against the reading), or a line on T2d3's review item. Effort: S.

### R3-6 [risk] A landing with a facing past 7 is played as a plain step, the hero left in its boat
