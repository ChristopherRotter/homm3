## Findings

### R1-1 [bug] A record whose viewer does not act first is played one turn late throughout
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:406` (`TurnStarted { player } if *player == viewer => starts =
true`), `:228` (the first observation taken as the viewer's own turn, no test of who acts).
What: every player's record starts with its `GameSnapshot` at the game's start
(`h3-protocol` `session/game.rs:606`: "every player gets its `GameSnapshot`, then each that may
act its `GameTurn`"), so `player-1.jsonl` (Blue, Red first) starts during Red's turn and holds
Blue's own day-1 `TurnStarted` after Red's `TurnEnded`. `played` makes a `[[turn]]` table of it,
although its doc (`Played::turns`, design §5.13) says "each of the viewer's `TurnStarted`
**after the first**". The timeline meanwhile plays the start as Blue's turn (the director gives
Blue's first walk at once). So Blue's first End Turn shows Red's day-1 turn (kind 5, if seen),
then day 1 again; every later day shows the turn before, and the last table is never reached.
Silent: nothing refuses it. Evidence: `GameObservation.players[i].acting` and `turn_group` exist
and are read nowhere in `h3-map-view` (`grep turn_group\|acting` empty); `session_record` and
every engine test use `PlayerId::new(0)`; `h3map.py engine_game` returns `player-0.jsonl` only,
but `h3-server record` writes every player's and `window --record` takes any file.
Suggested fix: refuse at `engine::load`/`played` a first observation whose viewer is not
`acting` (a new `Refused` variant), or skip the viewer's `TurnStarted` until its first and play
the turns before it as other players' turns from the start. Effort: S (refuse) / M (play).

### R1-2 [bug] `Loop::sees` reads only the start's explored tiles, so a computer's step is taken unseen in land the viewer explored since
Where: `src/timeline.rs:3414` (`sees`: `self.hover...explored`), used at `:3163` (`ai_hero`: the
view centred and drawn), `:3198` (`computer_step`'s `seen`), `:3315` (`ai_take`'s dissolve).
What: `self.hover` is set once from `Start::hover` (`:2010`) and never replaced; the viewer's
walks' reveals go to `walked_tiles` (`:5968`), which `sees` does not read; the `[[turn]]` tables
carry no explored tiles. So once the viewer has explored past the start's area, a computer's step
there gets no frames and no time (the hero jumps), its hero is not centred, and its pickup is
taken off with no dissolve. On a record this can only go wrong: every step in `OtherTurn` is one
the engine already judged seen (design §5.16: the record "holding only the events the viewer
sees ... the original's `step_seen`"). The recorded cases avoid it (§5.16: `game-ai`'s Blue
"its land all in Red's sight" at the start). The same stale bits give the "unexplored" arrow over
newly explored tiles (`over_map`, `:5119`; the hover's reviewer may list it too).
Suggested fix: on a record trust the record (a step is seen unless `unseen`, Blackout apart);
for Part A, and for the hero's selection, `sees` also reads `walked_tiles`; reveals made outside
a walk (`GameReveal` after a visit) need the turn tables to carry the explored tiles (or an
`MCL-Q`). Effort: S (records) / M (the explored tiles carried).

### R1-3 [bug] Two commands of one computer hero become one walk: the first move's pickup comes after the second move, and the first move's end is lost
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:517` (`other_event`: a step joins the last walk whenever the hero
is the same, whatever the command), `:549` (the command's `ObjectRemoved` pushed onto that
walk's `taken`); `src/timeline.rs:3066-3069` (`ai_on`: `last` and `beside` only at the run's
last step), `ai.taken` taken only after every step.
What: the record holds "one `GameEvents` message per command" (design §5.16), and the original
ends each move with `hero_move_step`'s last step (its fifth argument 1: stand, view drawn, the
objects stepped; §5.18). A hero that takes a pile and walks on in the same turn (any bot with
points left after the 101 a pile costs) is drawn walking past the pile, which only dissolves at
the end of its second move, beside nothing (`Direction::between` none: no turn to it); the
first move's stand and its `last_drawn` are skipped. The unit test
`a_record_s_other_turn_as_walks` builds two consecutive walks of hero 7 by hand, which `played`
never emits. Neither recorded game has a hero move twice.
Suggested fix: keep each command's move apart in `Steps` (its last steps' places and the
objects taken after each) so `ai_on` stands, draws and takes at every move's end; splitting the
walk at a command's first step (`!other.stepped`) is simpler but re-selects the hero (`ai_hero`
with `again` steps the objects), which the original's single `ai_hero_turn` may not (unsure: not
read). Effort: M.

### R1-4 [risk] The kingdom's slots are mapped through the start's lists: after a hero leaves or is hired the director chooses the wrong hero, and the next day selects the wrong map hero
Where: `src/director.rs:150` (`slots` = `Played::slots`, the start's: `played.rs:256`);
`src/timeline.rs:3427` (`map_hero`: `hover.facts().hero_list`, the start's), `:2916` and
`:5028` (`selected_id` likewise).
What: `after_battle`'s `Lost` arm removes the hero's slot from `kingdom.heroes` and
`chosen.heroes` (`timeline.rs` ~4380), and each new day's kingdom is the engine's, in the panels'
order (types', then the map's); a hired hero can also land before others. The start's slot of a
later hero then names another hero: `Action::Choose(slot)` picks it and `walk_to` refuses
(`NoPath`) at best; at a new day `turn_choice` maps `Selected::Hero(i)` through the start's
`hero_list` and can select the lost hero's map index (silent). Not on the review list (its T4c
record-player item names the waits; `Played::slots`' doc names only a hired hero).
Suggested fix: give each `Command::Walk` its slot from `slots(&observation, viewer)` of the
observation folded at that command; carry the hero id (or tile) in the turn table's hero rows so
the day's selection does not go through the start's `hero_list`. Effort: M.

### R1-5 [risk] A computer's other visible results are dropped or shown as a pickup
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:508-556` (`other_event`: only `HeroStepped`, `Boarded`, `Landed`,
`ObjectRemoved`); `live::Turn` (no owners).
What: a mine or dwelling a computer flags in sight (`ObjectFlagged`) keeps its old flag on the
view and minimap for the rest of the record (`Draw::owners` changes only by `raise` from the
viewer's own visits); any `ObjectRemoved` after a walk is dissolved as a pickup with the
pickup's sound (`object_type` ignored), so a monster a computer beats would look like a pile
taken; a computer hero lost in sight (`HeroRemoved`) stays drawn. Nothing names these: no
`MCL-Q`, no stub, not on the review list (unsure: whether every one reaches a viewer's record;
flags are public in the original).
Suggested fix: at least a stub or `MCL-Q`; fold `ObjectFlagged` of another turn into the walk
(`Steps`'s owners at its end), dissolve only the pickup classes and refuse the rest
(`Unplayable`) until built. Effort: M.

### R1-6 [bug] An object a computer takes without a step in a turn that has walks goes at that turn's start, before its walks
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:437` (`table(&mut taken, &mut untaken, turns.len())` right after
the computer's own table is pushed); `src/timeline.rs` `computer_start`'s `take_off(&taken)`.
What: MCL-Q46 (c) says "taken off the map as the next turn the record shows starts"; here a
pickup from beside in a turn that also has a seen walk is given to that turn's own table, so it
vanishes before the walks that preceded it. Only `game-ai-boat`'s day 3 (no seen walk) is
covered. Suggested fix: do not call `table()` at the computer's `TurnEnded`; the next table
pushed takes them. Effort: S.

### R1-7 [doc] Two doc comments still describe T1b2's catch-up that T4c2 replaced
Where: `src/timeline.rs:2813` (`turn_end`: "a computer's turn in sight, whose end then redraws
for each step it missed"), `:3328` (`ai_end`: "the redraws missed caught up").
What: `ai_end` now steps the objects once and moves the deadline at least a step on (its body,
the review list's T4c2 item: "Replaces T1b2's catch-up"). Suggested fix: reword both. Effort: S.

### R1-8 [nit] A computer hero's first step into sight in a later turn faces as at the record's start
Where: `src/turn.rs:337` (`record_walks`: `facings` is per `OtherTurn`, so a hero's first walk
falls back to the live part at 0's facing); `src/timeline.rs:3199-3201` (`computer_step` takes
`to.facing` for an unseen step).
What: the doc says "facing as before"; a hero last seen in an earlier turn appears facing its
start's way. Suggested fix: for an `unseen` step keep the timeline's hero's facing
(`Hero { at: to.at, ..self.hero }`). Effort: S.

### R1-9 [test] The record cases above have no test
What: no test of a record for a viewer other than player 0 (R1-1); of one computer hero's two
commands with a pickup between them (R1-3: `played` and the timeline); of a computer's step seen

walk of a later slot or a new day (R1-4); of a stepless pickup in a turn with walks (R1-6).
Effort: S each once the behaviour is decided.
