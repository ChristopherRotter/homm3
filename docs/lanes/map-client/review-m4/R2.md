## Findings

### R2-1 [bug] A record's later visits to one tile: only the last one's box kept, the rest dropped or unreachable
Where: `src/bin/h3-map-view/paint.rs:1290-1295` (`record_finds`, `Visited::Find`),
`paint.rs:1313-1350` (`Visited::Ask`), `src/timeline.rs:3775-3791` (`tell_find`), `timeline.rs:3975`
(`beside`).
What: the adapter makes one `Found` per tile from the record's first visit. Three things then go
wrong whenever the record visits a tile more than once.
(a) Each later `Find` visit overwrites `earlier.again` (`earlier.again = boxed; continue;`), and
`tell_find` shows that one `again` at every later visit. With three visits, the second shows the
third's box. Example: a Windmill taken again a week later, then found empty the same week.
(b) A later visit's `gain`, `flag`, `taken` and level-ups are thrown away. `again` opens as
`Told::Plain`, whose close goes straight to the idle step. So a second hero's first Star Axis,
Temple or Learning Stone shows its box but not its Spell Power, morale or experience (the latter
with no level-up dialog). A mine flagged again after an enemy took it shows MINEEVNT's box, but
its flag is not raised.
(c) `Visited::Ask` never merges. It pushes a second `Found` at the same `at`, and the timeline
never reaches it: `tell_find` and `beside` both take the first by `position`.
- A question answered once (`answered.contains(&i)`) tells nothing at a later visit, so a dwelling
  hired every week shows no box after the first. The director then passes the record's `Answer`
  over as "no box open".
- A Pandora's Box first declined has its `yes` pruned (`Answered::No`: `question.yes = None`).
  When the record opens it later, the first `Found` asks again, Enter reaches `answer` with
  `yes == None`, and since a record is played with `live: false`, `unbuilt` returns
  `Err(Unplayable::Answer)`. The record stops.

Evidence: design §5.14 says only "a find's later visit its `again` box". The scripted games have
one later visit with no gain (`game-finds`' Temple) and no question asked twice.
Suggested fix: keep the record's visits in order. Make one `Found` per visit, and have the
timeline take the next visit not yet played at the hero's tile (or make `again` a queue of
per-visit boxes, each with its gain, flag and answers). Part A's single `again` can stay as it is.
Effort: M.

### R2-2 [bug] The record player cannot answer a choice box (a chest, the Arena, a School)
Where: `src/director.rs:117-125` (`answer => … _ => Key::Ok`); `h3-ui/src/message.rs:977-986`
(`Open::key`: "A disabled button takes no key (a choice's OK before its choice)");
`src/timeline.rs:3535` (`Action::Offer` acts only where `offers` is a level-up's).
What: for `Answer::Chest` and `Answer::Arena` the director presses Enter. A style 7 or 10 box keeps
its OK disabled until a picture is picked, so the key does nothing. `record_finds` has also pruned
the answers that are not the record's, which leaves no Cancel for the chest or for the Arena's
Defense. The box stays open. On every later wait the director presses Enter again: the next
command is not an `Answer`, so it falls into `_ => None`. This goes on to the limit and ends in
`Err((limit, Unplayable::Record([-1, -1, -1])))`.
Evidence: the review list's T4c item says "the other choices Enter", but that cannot close these
boxes. The director's tests answer only End Turn's question (`timeline/tests/director.rs:205-249`),
and the chest answers in its other tests are given with no box open. The sequences are not
affected (`game-asks` plays the recorded clicks on the pictures).
Suggested fix: for a chest or Arena answer, give a pick first (a click at the chosen picture's item
point, or a new `Action::Pick(n)` that `Open` handles as its press), then Enter. Add a director
test with a chest's and the Arena's box open. Effort: S–M.

### R2-3 [contract] Part B decides the level-ups in the client, not from the record
Where: `src/timeline.rs:3660-3675` (`levels_due`: `level_of(experience)`, then
`level_ups.iter().find(|b| b.level == level)`), `timeline.rs:4477` (`gain`: experience only when
`self.index == self.experienced`); `paint.rs:1638-1643` (`level_ups`: only the selected hero's
type); `paint.rs:~417` (`chosen` found by `(level, offers)` over every hero's `Leveled`).
What: the record already says which visit leveled whom and which offer was taken (`Played::levels`
from `LevelUpOpened` and `LevelGained`). The viewer still works out which levels open from the
start experience plus the visits' `GainExperience`, using the tables' thresholds and its own
integer ×1.2 rule past level 12. It then takes a dialog by level number.
- Any experience the timeline does not add puts it out of step. Examples: a second hero (the
  `experienced` index is the hero at 0 only, and the adapter drops every other hero's dialogs), a
  later visit's gain (R2-1 b), and a battle whose result is `AfterBattle::Stays`.
  `after_battle`'s `_ =>` arm calls `gain` without `level_up`, so that battle's levels open at the
  next find instead.
- Any difference between the engine's levels and `level_of` leads to `Unplayable::LevelUp` or to
  a missing dialog.

Evidence: the brief: "a fact the record lacks becomes an MCL-Q, never a rule copied into the
client". Here the record does not lack the fact. `levels.rs`' module doc says "Part B takes them
from the engine", but only the dialog's facts come from it.
Suggested fix: on a record, attach each visit's `Leveled` (in record order, with the hero) to its
`Found` or `Answer` and open exactly those. Keep `level_of` for Part A's recordings. Effort: M.

### R2-4 [risk] Part B's Pandora's Box shows only some of its rewards and ignores the map's message
Where: `src/engine/visits.rs:347-357` (`asked`: `Rewards` built from `experience`, `resources` and
`primary` alone); `visits.rs:422-460` (`gain` reads only `ResourceGained`, `GainPrimary`,
`GainExperience`, `OnceMarkChanged`); compare `src/asks.rs:661` (Part A: `PANDORA if
seen.message.is_none()`, a message or guards not built).
What:
- A Box that gives artifacts, spells, creatures, spell points, morale, luck or secondary skills
  shows its `grant_rewards` boxes without those parts. A Box that gives only those shows no box at
  all, because `finds::rewards` emits nothing for an empty part. The Box is still taken.
- A Box with the map's message (or guards) is told with ADVEVENT 14's question. Part A refuses
  that case as not built; Part B shows it wrong, silently.

Evidence: `game-asks`' Box gives 1 500 gold only, so the recorded case cannot show this.
Suggested fix: fold `AddArtifact`, the creatures joined, the spells learned and the mana into
`Rewards`, or refuse the Box (and record an MCL-Q for what the record lacks). Leave out a Box with a
message or guards as Part A does. Effort: S–M.

### R2-5 [bug] A record's accepted hire at a class-20 dwelling is told as a class-17 one
Where: `src/engine/visits.rs:284` (`DWELLING | DWELLINGS => hired(visit, subclass, flag)`) →
`Ask::Hire { dwelling: subclass }` → `asks.rs:851-857` (`map.ask.hire`, ADVEVENT 35, with
`family(texts, "dwelling1", subclass)`, CrGen1's names of class 17).
What: a class-20 dwelling's hire is told with ADVEVENT 35 and the class-17 dwelling of the same
subclass number. That gives the wrong name in the text, and the original uses another record.
Evidence: `asks.rs:34`, *Not built*: "a dwelling of class 20 (ADVEVENT 36)". Its test covers only a
declined class-20 hire (`tests/engine.rs`, `what_a_record_s_flags_tell`: `told(&declined, 20, 0)
== None`). This is T4b2's code, inside `visits.rs`.
Suggested fix: return `None` for class 20 until ADVEVENT 36 and its names are built, and add the
accepted case to the test. Effort: S.

### R2-6 [risk] Part B's artifact pickup leaves out the artifact's primary skills on the hero's window
Where: `src/engine/visits.rs:262-270` (`ARTIFACT`: `Told::Find`'s `gain` is `visits::gain(events)`,
which reads no `AddArtifact` effect); compare `src/finds.rs:716-719` (Part A: `skills_of(artifact)`
from the tables' `artifact_skills`).
What: picking up a primary-skill artifact (Centaur's Axe +2 Attack, and so on) changes the status
window in the original and in Part A. A record shows the hero's old skills.
Evidence: `game-finds` picks the Pendant of Dispassion "which no panel shows" because of MCL-Q36
(morale and luck). Primary skills are the same gap, but neither MCL-Q36 nor the review item names
them.
Suggested fix: name it in MCL-Q36 (the hero's skills after the pickup from the record), or apply
Part A's `skills_of` (the packs' data) to the record's `AddArtifact`. Effort: S.
