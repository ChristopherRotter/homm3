### R5-1 [bug] The record player walks onto a monster's tile: no switch, no result, the hero one tile off
Where: `src/director.rs:147-155` (`direct`, `Command::Walk`), `src/timeline.rs:2450` (`walk_to`),
`:5306` (`walk_path`), `:5445` (`carry_on`), `:3769` (`tell_find`), `:5236` (`key_step`, the only
caller of `beside`). `src/bin/h3-map-view/paint.rs:1191-1225` (`record_finds`, `Visited::Fight`).
What: the engine's attack on a wandering monster is a `MoveHero` whose path ends on the monster's
tile (`sequences.toml` `battle-won`: `script = ["0 move 0 5,4,0", …]`, the monster on (5, 4)).
`record_finds` turns it into a `Found` with `blocks: Some(o)` and the `fight`. But the record
player only ever gives `Action::Choose` and `Action::Walk(to)` (the path's last tile, `command_of`
at `played.rs:490`). `walk_to` then walks every arrow up to `GREEN`, the `DESTINATION` arrow (1)
on the monster's tile included. No step of a path walk checks `beside` (only `key_step` does,
`:5251`). So the hero is animated onto the monster's tile, `finish` hands the battle over as a
plain cut (`:5534`, since `switched` does not hold it), and `tell_find` skips the find
(`.position(|f| f.at == at && f.blocks.is_none())`). The result is that for a record (both
`record` and `window --record`) T5b2's whole switch and result never run: no fades, no
`killfade`, a beaten monster still drawn with the hero standing on it, and a retreated hero left
on the map. The next walk of that hero starts from the engine's end tile (`record_paths` uses
`Played::ends`), but the viewer's hero is on the monster's tile. `walk_to`'s
`in_order(hero.at, p)` then fails when the two tiles are not neighbours, which would refuse the
whole record with `Unplayable::NoPath` (unsure: not run). Only the sequences' key steps (`Up` at
100) reach `visit_beside`, so the three T5b2 checks pass. The golden replays checked at T5b1 seem
to fight zone-of-control guards (the path ends on the visited object, e.g. the stone of
`rewards-stone-guard-s1`), so their walks never end on a class-54 tile.
Evidence: design §5.14 already says "a visit from beside on a record takes the record's next
move, its one tile the object's", so only a one-tile key step is handled. §5.21 says a record's
class-54 visit is `Told::Fight` with its switch. The exit criterion: "a golden adventure replay
… viewable from start to end in the window with … its battles".
Suggested fix: on a record walk, a path whose last arrow is a find with `blocks` should stop one
tile short and end with `visit_beside`, the way the computer's walk already does ("a walk that
ends taking an object from beside has the object's node last, which takes no step", §5.18). Add a
director test that walks into a `Fight` find. Once that works, the stale `Played::slots` become
reachable (R5-6's note).
Effort: M.

### R5-2 [bug] A mine's guards' battle on a record is handed over twice
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:357-360` (`Command::Battle` after an answer), `src/director.rs:129-132`
(`Directed::battles` at the release), `src/timeline.rs:3877-3880` (`answered` → `switch_out`),
`:4270` (`self.battles.push` at the switch's whole battle), `src/bin/h3-map-view/window.rs:974-1030`,
`records.rs:129`.
What: since T5b2, a mine's `Told::Guarded` answer carries its `Fight` (`paint.rs:1229-1266`). That
battle is now listed twice:
- in `Directed::battles` (`answered`), at the answer box's key release;
- in `Loop::battles`, 250 ms later, where the switch shows the battle whole.

The window hosts it at the release first, as a plain cut before the map has even faded. Its
`BattleClosed` at `t + 1` arrives in `Phase::Out` and is dropped (`timeline.rs:2213`: only
`Phase::Battle` takes it). Then the map fades out and the battle fades in from its first frame.
At the whole frame the window finds `replay.battles()` grown and hosts the same battle a second
time. `record` prints it twice ("battles handed over: …").
Reachable today: `economy-guards-s1` (Blue's record, listed as checked at T5b1, before T5b2) and
`economy-abandoned-s1` (Red's).
Evidence: `Setup::battles`' doc ("a walk's are the timeline's own, `Replay::battles`") and design
§5.20 (an answer's battle "where the answer's box closes") both predate §5.21's switch for answers
("at the hand-over (… an answer's box closed …) … whole at 250 (… the battle handed over then:
`Loop::battles`)").
Suggested fix: do not push `Command::Battle` / `Directed::battles` for an answer whose find
carries the fight. Simpler: make every hand-over `Loop::battles`' and have an answer's plain-cut
battle push there too, so the host reads one list. Re-run `record` over T5b1's golden list.
Effort: S.

### R5-3 [bug] The record player neither waits through a switch nor closes its battle
Where: `src/timeline.rs:2482-2491` (`Loop::idle` has no `switching` term), `src/director.rs:87-171`
(no `Action::BattleClosed` anywhere), `src/timeline.rs:2209-2217` (every action but the close
dropped during a switch).
What: during a battle's switch, `idle()` is true and `boxed()` is false. So 400 ms after the
switch starts, the director gives the record's next commands, and `run` drops them. A walk's
`Choose`/`Walk` is lost, End Turn is lost, and a level-up's `Answer` is "passed over" in the
no-box branch. The director never gives `BattleClosed`, so in its own run (the one that computes
`actions`) the switch stays in `Phase::Battle` to the end. The consequences:
- `direct` returns `Ok` (or `Untaken(n)` if a walk follows), and `record` prints "viewed to its
  end" while the map stands on the switch's black.
- In the window, the host's `BattleClosed` lets the switch go on, but the inputs were laid out
  for a map that never came back. ADVEVENT 85's box, MINEEVNT's, and the level-up after
  `economy-abandoned-s1`'s battle open with no answer coming, and the record's End Turn arrives
  while a box is open.

Today only mines take the switch on a record (R5-1). Fixing R5-1 makes every monster fight take
it too.
Suggested fix: count `switching` in `Loop::idle` (or add `Loop::battling`). Have the director give
`Action::BattleClosed` once `Loop::battles` grows, at `t + 1`, the window's own; the window's give
then becomes redundant, or it keeps it for a battle the user closes later. Test: the director over
a mine's `Guarded` find and its level-up.
Effort: M.

### R5-4 [bug] A mine's guards beaten the hero still shows "the guards beaten" and the mine's box, and keeps the hero
Where: `src/bin/h3-map-view/paint.rs:1229-1266` (`Visited::Guarded` always `AfterBattle::Boxes`,
the boxes always ADVEVENT 85 and MINEEVNT), `src/timeline.rs:4354-4360` (`after_battle`'s
`Boxes` ignores `fight.winner`).
What: `Told::Guarded` carries `winner`, but the find's answer is built the same way whatever it
says. When the record's hero loses to the guards or retreats (winner 1, `HeroRemoved`),
`after_battle` still gains the experience and opens `map.find.mine-guards` (ADVEVENT 85, "the
guards beaten") and MINEEVNT's box. The hero is not taken off the map. A wrong state carried
from a record input. The flag is right, since none comes without `ObjectFlagged`.
A smaller case: in `Told::Fight` a draw that removes both the monster and the hero maps
`(taken, lost) = (true, true)` to `Taken` alone (`paint.rs:1218-1222`). The hero stays, and
§5.19 says `battle_aftermath` takes both.
Evidence: §5.19: "A guarded mine (`visit_mine`): ADVEVENT 85's box (the guards beaten), then §5.6's
MINEEVNT box and the flag"; "the loser hero leaves … an attacking hero beaten or retreated (winner
1) … through `hero_loss_effect` 0".
Suggested fix: for `winner != 0`, use `AfterBattle::Lost` (no boxes, no flag) when `HeroRemoved`
is among `battle.ended`. Carry `lost` in `Told::Guarded` as `Told::Fight` does. Add a unit test
with winner 1.
Effort: S.

### R5-5 [bug] The map's focus and mouse state go stale across a hosted battle
Where: `src/bin/h3-map-view/window.rs:634` (`let mut focused = true`), `:837-855`;
`src/bin/h3-map-view/battles.rs:211-228`.
What: `Window::focus_changes()` holds only the last `wait`'s changes (`h3-sdl/src/lib.rs:655-662`,
cleared at `:713`). During the battle, `battles::pass` calls `wait`, so every focus change in the
battle is seen there alone. The map loop's `focused` keeps its old value:
- focus lost during the battle and not regained: the map's next regain is dropped
  (`has_focus == focused`), so the sound is not resumed and the edge scroll stays live without
  the focus;
- focus lost before the battle and regained during it: `focused` stays false, so
  `pointer = None` drops every mouse move after the battle until the focus changes twice.

The map's `mouse` also keeps its pre-battle point (the battle took the moves), so the mouse-over
after the battle is the old point's until the mouse moves (§5.19's `adventure_mouse_again` is
"where the mouse stands").
A related gap in `pass`: the battle is not told a focus already lost at its start (`focused =
win.focused()`, no `hosted.focus(false)`). Its effects then play unfocused; the shared music is
right, since the map told `HostSound`.
Suggested fix: after `battles::host`, set `focused = win.focused()` and give
`Action::Pointer(OFF)` or the window's current point as the map loop's own focus code does
(`player.focus` is not needed again: the battle passed each change to `HostSound`). In `pass`,
call `hosted.focus(false)` at the start when the window lacks the focus.
Effort: S.

### R5-6 [bug] A battle's commands cost the map 400 ms each and stand between a walk and its boxes' answers
Where: `src/engine/played.rs:490-501` (`command_of`: every battle command `Command::Other`),
`src/director.rs:105-111` (one command looked ahead), `:167-169` (`Other` after a full wait).
What: a battle is handed over whole, but its commands stay in `Played::commands` as `Other`, and
each takes the director's 400 ms wait with nothing shown. `teleporters-guard-s1` has 5 battle
commands, so the map stands 2 s after its battle. Blue's `economy-guards-s1` has about 18, so
about 7 s.
They also sit between the walk and the answers to the boxes the walk opens (the level-up after
a battle, `rewards-stone-guard-s1`'s `answer level-up first` after `battle attack 7 116`). A box

offer unchosen is refused (`timeline.rs:3722-3731`). Unsure: T4c played `rewards-stone-guard-s1`
to its end, so the dialog there must open once the commands are spent; a record whose dialog opens
at the walk's end, or whose answer is `second`, would not play.
Note for R5-1's fix: `Played::slots` (`played.rs:579`) are the start's, so a hero gone after a
retreat (`after_battle` removes its slot) shifts every later `Choose(slot)`.
Suggested fix: in `engine::played`, leave out the commands accepted between a handed-over
battle's `BattleStarted` and its `BattleEnded`, or mark them `Command::InBattle` and skip them with

Effort: S.

### R5-7 [risk] The battle's first frame can flash between its end and its fade out (unsure)
Where: `src/bin/h3-map-view/window.rs:1015` (`BattleClosed` at `t + 1`), `:1027`
(`clock.seek(…, t)`), `src/timeline.rs:4262-4271` (the change at `t` is the battle whole from its
first frame).
What: the battle is hosted before the map's change at `t`, and that change is the switch's
`Switch { battle, shift: 0, ended: false }`, drawn from `battles::first_frame`. After the battle
the clock is put back to `t` and the close given at `t + 1`. Unless the next iteration's `ms` has
already passed `t`, the change at `t` (still `next`) is presented: the battle's opening position,
for at least one refresh, between its last frame and the fade out that starts from that last
frame. Whether it shows depends on timing; under `--check`, `log` would hold the `t` change.
Suggested fix: give the close at `t` (`switch_step` sets `Phase::Battle` before the actions are
read, so the change at `t` becomes the `ended` one), or drop `next` when it is the switch's whole
frame being replaced.
Effort: S.

### R5-8 [risk] The map's ambient loops stay silent after a battle
Where: `src/bin/h3-map-view/audio.rs:391-394` (`lend` → `stop_effects` clears every lane's clip),
`:399-403` (`take_back` restores gains only), `src/sound.rs:654-697` (`ambient`: a slot it believes
playing gets `LoopVolume` only).
What: the timeline's sound model is not told that the host stopped its loops. After the battle,
its next ambient pass sends `Cue::LoopVolume` for the slots it still holds. `Player::cue` sets the
gain of a lane with no clip, and `service` queues only lanes with a clip, so a windmill's or a
river's loop near the hero stays off until it leaves the four slots and is found again. The
original's return (`adventure_full_update`, design §5.19) was not read for the ambient slots, and
the exit criterion's hand test is "the map's sound by ear".
Suggested fix: on `BattleClosed` (or at `map_back` / a plain cut's hand-over) have the sound model
forget its slots and step, so the next pass starts them again. Or ask it as an `MCL-Q` with the
original's reading first.
Effort: S.
