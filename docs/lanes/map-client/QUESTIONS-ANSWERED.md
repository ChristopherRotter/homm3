- *(2026-10-27, MCL-Q11, E24 M2 T4b3; answered at M2 T6a's start)* **Nine step sounds ship but never play: the client plays the original's (00 to 10 only); the unused set becomes a sound option once its purpose is known.** The adventure manager loads its step sounds once, `horse%02d.wav` for 0 to 10 (`adventure_manager_start` 0x40747A): by the hero's terrain 0 to 9 (a boat the water's) and 10 for a hero flying (`docs/epics/E24-design.md` §3.11). `Heroes3.snd` also holds `HORSE20` to `HORSE27` and `HORSE29`, each longer than its 00 to 09 counterpart (6.7 KB against 5.4 KB of IMA ADPCM); there is no `HORSE28`, 8 being the water's index, where no road runs, so they may be the roads' sounds (*inferred*: nothing in the executable names them; its only `horse` string is the loader's). The user (2026-10-27): "there are a few terrain-specific samples which the original doesn't seem to play for some reason, it just plays what seems like the wrong ones". The 20 files are in the user's `clients/out/win/horses/` to listen to. **Options:** (a) the client plays as the original (00 to 10 only; as built); (b) the client plays 2x on a road of terrain x (the intent guessed), the original's for the comparisons; (c) (b) as a sound option, the original's by default. **Recommendation:** (a) now, (c) once the user's ear or a source settles what 2x were meant for. **The user (2026-10-27):** these are the sounds meant; recommendation taken, (a) now and (c) later. Asked with it: the original's direction-key steps are silent (the step sounds play only on a clicked path's walk, `docs/epics/E24-design.md` §3.11); the user does not remember hooves there: kept silent.
- *(2026-10-27, MCL-Q10, E24 M2 T4b2; asked and answered in the same task)* **A held direction key walks the hero on after its release: kept as the original's, the inconvenience noted.** The original queues every WM_KEYDOWN, a repeat's too (`input_key_message` 0x4EC0E0; `docs/epics/E24-design.md` §3.4), and the waits of a step's frames dispatch the window's messages (`wait_until` 0x4F82F0), so the repeats Windows sends while a step runs (about 30 a second against a step of 100 to 200 ms) wait in the input manager's ring and each is a step once the one before ends: the hero walks on after the key's release until they are used up (the ring keeps 63 messages, the oldest dropped) or its points run out. The user, trying T4b1's live input on Windows, saw it walk on well past the release (the client's queue has no bound and no points yet). Options were (a) the client drops a repeat while a step waits, so the hero stops at most a step after the release, the original's queue for the comparisons, (b) keep the original's, (c) an option. The user (2026-10-27): **keep it for now** (b), noting the inconvenience; in the original Space cancels it, perhaps other means too (not read: the adventure screen's keys, M3); the ring's bound and the points come with M3's keys and M4's points.
- *(2026-10-26, MCL-Q6, E24 M2 T1a2; answered at T1b's start)* **The edge scroll's zones are 16, 14, 16 and 15 pixels wide: the client's are 16 on every side, the comparisons take the original's.** The map scrolls while the mouse lies within the screen's edge zones (`edge_scroll` 0x419820; `docs/epics/E24-design.md` §3.4): x < 16 west, x > 784 east, y < 16 north, y > 584 south, on the mouse's point as `mouse_position_now` 0x50D500 gives it, x rounded down to even (E17's drawing bug), so the south zone is 15 pixels wide and the east one 14; the mouse-move's test that keeps an edge cursor (0x40EBBA) is the same. A pixel or two, harmless to play. Options were (a) the client's zones 16 pixels on every side (x < 16, x ≥ 784, y < 16, y ≥ 584 on the mouse's own point), the original's tests on its rounded point for the comparisons only (`h3_render::Bugs`, as MCL-Q2 to Q5), (b) the original's tests on the unrounded point (16, 15, 16, 15), (c) the original's with the rounding. The user took (a) (2026-10-26): T2 builds the even zones by default and the original's behind `--bugs original`.
- *(2026-10-24, MCL-Q5, E24 M1 T6a; answered in the same task)* **The rivers' colour cycle can run a step behind the terrains' for a whole game: the viewer keeps them in step, the comparisons take the original's two steps.** The adventure manager loads the map's DEFs kind by kind with a step of the loading screen's progress bar between the kinds (adventure_manager_start 0x406FD0, load_progress_step 0x4ED2A0); showing the bar runs the palette tick (screen_show_rect 0x602BD0, adventure_tick 0x4ED490), and the load runs in real time, so a tick due at the step after the terrains rotates water and lava before the rivers are loaded, and the rivers' cycles run one step behind for the whole game (`docs/epics/E24-design.md` §1.6; about one still in seven, the inbox's "half tick"). Nothing a player can tell. Options were (a) the viewer keeps them in step on its own clock (`Moment::at`) and only the comparisons take the original's two steps from its memory (`live.toml`'s `river_cycles`), (b) the viewer copies it in a share of games (a draw where the original has a race), (c) a graphics option. The user took (a), as built.

- *(2026-10-23, MCL-Q3, E24 M1 T4b; answered in the same task)* **A random artifact is drawn as the original's copy of its random template: the client draws the artifact's own picture, the comparisons reproduce the original.** A new game gives a random object its picture by copying its template and replacing only the DEF, class and subclass (object_template_redraw 0x4C9990; `docs/epics/E24-design.md` §2.7): the copy keeps the random template's masks, which cut the Crown of Dragontooth (2 body pixels) and the combination artifacts (10 to 584), and an artifact's DEF is `ava%04d` of its id, which from 128 on is the editor's picture of the artifact before it (a random relic become Angelic Alliance shows Armageddon's Blade, Armageddon's Blade an unused box). Monsters and resources draw the same either way. Options were (a) the client draws the artifact's own picture and masks and the comparisons reproduce the original (`h3_render::Bugs`), (b) copy it always, (c) a graphics option. The user took the recommendation (a): `render` draws the game's last template of the class and subclass (`h3_map_view::live::Drawn::own`), `--bugs original` the copy.
