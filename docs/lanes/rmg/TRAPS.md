# RMG lane — standing traps

Grouped by area. Read the group for the area your task touches; delete a trap when it stops applying, merge duplicates, add a new one under its area (a trap of every lane goes into `docs/HANDOFF.md` or the topic doc).

## Reading the executable

- The generator's own range is 0x530EE0–0x54D0F0, but its closure calls about 250 game functions outside it (text tables, `template_file_read`, `msk_load`, object tables): many are already in `docs/exe/map.toml`, so `lookup` before reading.
- H3API's names for the generator (`H3RmgRandomMapGenerator`, `H3RmgMap`, `H3RmgObject`) come from the 3.2 build shifted by -0x4F0 in this region: a pointer where to read, never a rule's source (`docs/exe/README.md`, "The H3API port").
- The seed is `time()` in seconds (0x536148); the Random choices are drawn earlier with the same `rand` (0x586168): an oracle case with a Random choice depends on the state of `rand` before the generation.
- The call closure misses the methods reached only through vtables: of the range's 255 functions, 154 are in the closure of 0x54BF00 and 0x54C090 by direct calls, 101 (about 16 kB) only in vtables (the objects' and object generators' methods). Find them from the vtables (`refs` on a vtable's address).
- The C runtime's `rand` keeps its state per thread (`crt_rand`): the generation and the writing run on Begin's thread, one stream; the writer draws too (`rmg_teams_draw`, after every draw of the generation).
- `docs/exe/` is not scanned for citations: an address in a map summary needs no entry of its own, one in the survey or the lane's docs does (`cargo xtask check-exe-map`).
- Two of the generator's draws depend on memory, not on the seed alone (survey §2.6): step 2's uninitialised town-type bytes and step 15's read past the cells. A port that zeroes them can differ from the original: the phase oracle sees 0x532A5B draw in step 2 in 18 of 21 first maps, and a second map in one game differ by it (ORACLE.md, "The phase oracle"). The user's call (RMG-QUIRK-1, -2): `faithful` models a fresh start's first map under the oracle (the bytes measured by the phase hooks when step 2 is ported, kept as data), `fixed` zeroes them; never zero them in `faithful`.
- Survey §2.6 lists the draw sites of the generator's range only: a third of the stream comes from the game's terrain painter (steps 3, 6, 11) and its road and river painter (steps 14, 15), outside the range: §2.7 reads them and §2.7.5 gives their rows. A port draws them too, in the painters' cell order (row-major sets, the neighbours clockwise from north).
- Maps the user's game generated may come from an HD-mod-patched executable (`Untitled.h3m` differs from the plain writer in three points, survey §2.5): the oracle's maps come from the plain `Heroes3.exe`.

## The oracle

- A run directory without `random_maps/` makes Begin fail ("Cannot create Random_Map.H3M in data directory."): `h3rmg.py` makes it afresh per case; the shell's recipes have none.
- The seed is the date `xdd.dll` gives (`H3TIME`) only with `TZ=UTC` (the C library's `time()` converts the local date by its time zone): `h3shell.prepare` sets it; a run outside `h3rmg.py` must too. The description's seed is the check.
- The Random Map tab shows two levels at each opening of the scenario screen (its button toggles): press the levels only to change them from two.
- A template's name may hold a line break ("Meeting⏎in Muzgob", a quoted cell of `rmg.txt`): read the tables with a parser of quoted cells, quote names in kept lines.
- The oracle runs under Wine, whose fresh memory is not Windows': a draw that depends on uninitialised bytes can agree with itself under Wine and still differ on Windows.
- The original's heap layout, and with it step 2's uninitialised zone bytes and the map, follows the game's environment block (E14 T3a, ORACLE.md, "The memory record and the fixed environment"): the oracle runs with `h3drive.py`'s fixed environment (`H3_FIXED_ENV`, which `h3rmg.py` sets; its clock folder through a link of one length). A map, a `phases.txt` or `zones.txt` line made with the sandbox's own environment (`--sandbox-env`, every run before T3a) is not comparable: `m2-4p`, `l1-4p-b` and `xl2-4p-seed0` moved with it. A second map in one game (`--again`) can still differ from the first (`l1-4p-b`), by the heap's reuse: the original's, not the harness's.
- Two fresh starts agree on whether each of step 2's zone bytes is 0, not on their values (the temporary zone's stack bytes from +0x14, the zone generator's outside +8, water zones' heap bytes): compare `zones.txt`'s form (`h3rmg.zone_fields`), never the memory record's raw hex.
- The phase hooks (`H3RMG`, `xdd.c` section 10) must not write the game's stack below a call: the generator reads uninitialised stack bytes (step 2's temporary zone). They run on their own stack and enter `rand` after its own thread-data call; keep it so when changing them.
- `h3-pack-rmg`'s corpus tests read `phases.txt` and `layout.txt` (`include_str!`): `corpus --phases --record` rewrites both, so a re-record changes what CI checks; diff them before committing. A port's frame splits its draws before step 1 into the log's `setup` (water's Random, by its site) and `template` phases (E14 T3c).
- `water.txt` keeps no `town2`: a water zone's +0x8 is the heap's and differs between fresh starts (T3d); compare only what fresh starts agree on.
- The game ends at its start when the run folder's free bytes modulo 4 GiB are outside 5 MiB to 2 GiB (its 32-bit disk check; `h3rmg.py` refuses the case and names a ballast's size): `fallocate` a ballast outside the run folder for about 1 GiB, and start no build while the corpus runs (a build moves the free bytes by gigabytes; E14 T2c).

## The port's code

- `h3-rmg`'s gate is 100 % lines and regions: every `?` and every `if` needs both outcomes in a test. A fault the original would not survive and the corpus never reaches goes into the step's per-level record (`Level::fail`, checked once per level) or a documented fallback (the triangulation's), not a `?` at each call (T3d).
- A step's corpus test reads its own snapshot (`StepState` at its `step_end`): later steps append to `GeneratedMap`'s zone generators (step 2's water zones) and draw at step 1's sites (0x532A5B), so tests of step 1 slice the log at `ends[1]`.
- The terrain painter's water and rock branches (`set_cell`'s 8-neighbour re-test, the split ring's gaps) never run in step 3's corpus (water is painted over water, rock in one rectangle): only unit tests check them until steps 6 (islets, shores) and 11 (underground rock) reach them; a divergence there starts in those branches (survey §2.7.2, §2.7.3).
- The painter reads and writes the map, not the original's 16-bit copy: equal only while nothing else writes the map during a painter's life (true in step 3 and step 6: the islets' painter and the shores' seal's, whose marks all come before its painter is made; check step 11's `rmg_underground_rock_paint`, `terrain_painter_flush`'s other caller, before relying on it).
- Steps 4 on take a town type's prototype by its index in class 98's vector: a pack without the nine town lines fails at step 4 (`ObjectFault::Prototype`). The synthetic pack has invented ones since T4b; `h3-pack-rmg`'s `one_template` copies its object table, so a test template's zones may ask for towns.
- Since T4c every corpus test and the property test pay step 6's plan, which dominates on the islands maps: each painter `flush` runs the transition pass over the whole level, and the seal flushes once per change of land terrain (`xl2-8p-t4-islands`: 6 294 cells sealed, 497 flushes). It ran CI's coverage job past its 60 minutes (#1217's first run); the pass's two shortcuts (no shape at once among a cell's own terrain; a kept plain picture before the weight) took `h3-pack-rmg`'s tests from 354 s to 102 s in a debug build (`xl2-8p-t4-islands` 38 s to 11 s) and CI's coverage job to 39 minutes (T4b's: 27). Before adding a corpus test, or a step whose painter flushes (step 11), measure; the inbox holds the next ways.
