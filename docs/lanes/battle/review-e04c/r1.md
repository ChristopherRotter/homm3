## Findings

### F-r1-1: On Magic Plains the opening spells (caster kind 2) are not cast at Expert, against the reading, the ADR and the content's own doc
- rules: BAT-TER-005 (BAT-MAG-068)
- kind: behaviour-bug (and rule-text: the summary omits the opening spells while ADR-0022 point 5, `battlefield.rs:52` ("Creature, strike and opening spells are cast at Expert") and the catalog's own source line ("kind … 2 (an opening spell) takes its argument, Expert on field 1 only") include them)
- severity: low (every opening spell of the shipped packs is already at mastery 3: `packs/sod/core.toml:40,64`, the synthetic pack's too; a pack with an opening spell below Expert deviates)
- confidence: high. Re-read 0x59FE30 at 0x59FE98–0x59FECD: `test ecx, ecx` on the kind, kind 0 calls 0x4E5080, every other kind takes [ebp+0x18] and sets 3 when +0x53C0 == 1; §7.24 says the opening spells are cast through this routine with kind 2.
- evidence: `crates/h3-battle/src/magic.rs:1055-1073`: `mastery: o.mastery` and `spell.effect[usize::from(o.mastery)]`, no `other_mastery`; `other_mastery` is called only at `casters.rs:112,326` and `after_strike.rs:81`. Scenario: a hero with an opening spell at Basic (a modded pack) on Magic Plains → the original casts it at Expert (mass, the Expert value); the engine at Basic.
- fix: in `opening`, `let mastery = other_mastery(state, o.mastery)` and use it for `mastery`, `value` and the `in_reach` test; add the opening spells to BAT-TER-005's summary; a test with a synthetic hero whose opening spell has mastery 1 on `t:plains` (its effect value is the Expert one) and on `t:fiery` (unchanged).

### F-r1-2: The ported placement forbids an anchor in the obstacle's own row (`y <= height`), the original allows it (`row >= height`): row 1 never gets a usual obstacle in the engine
- rules: BAT-GEO-004 (the E03 base of the rule, found while re-reading the E04c amendment's routine)
- kind: source (the note "VCMI reproduces the original's tile-seeded generator" is not true of the position test) and behaviour-bug
- severity: medium (systematic in every battle with random obstacles: every usual obstacle sits at least one row higher than the original's lowest allowed row; row 1 is blocked only by absolute obstacles; the original can block row 1 with a height-1 or height-2 type), though obstacles are random and the engine never claimed seed equality
- confidence: high on the reading, medium on the gameplay weight. 0x466010 at 0x466072–0x46608F: row = pos / 17 (the 0x78787879 multiply), `cmp height, row; jg reject`, so accepted when row ≥ height; VCMI `BattleInfo.cpp:254` `if(obi.height >= pos.getY()) return false` (accepted when y > height); engine `terrain.rs:75` `if y <= i32::from(o.height) … return None`. The record's +4 is the height (type 0: +4 = 1, +5 = 2, VCMI width 2, height 1; the width byte +5 is the one tested against 15 at 0x4660A7). The rest of the original's test equals the port: x = pos % 17 ≠ 0 (0x466099), x + width ≤ 15 (0x4660A9), the anchor free (0x4660BD, flags 0x3F), each blocked hex: the odd-row shift (0x4660E5–0x4660FF), column 3..=13 (0x46610A–0x466116), free (0x46612B).
- fix: `terrain.rs:75` → `y < i32::from(o.height)`; a test with a height-1 type: anchor row 1 allowed, row 0 not (the sampler starts at 18 anyway). Needs the user's call whether the port follows the original or VCMI (the client's pictures are VCMI-agnostic). Also F-r1-6 below for the two other divergences.

### F-r1-6: The original draws usual types without repetition across the whole battle and keeps going after a type has no place; the port (as VCMI) may repeat a type and stops
- rules: BAT-GEO-004
- kind: source / behaviour-bug
- severity: low (shapes repeat in the engine where the original never shows one type twice; a battle where a drawn type has no free place ends its obstacles early in the engine, the original tries the next type; both rare or cosmetic)
- confidence: high. 0x4667F8 initialises the sampler (0..90) once before the loop; 0x46680B/0x46683C draw from it inside the loop, −1 ends it; 0x466858 `test al, al; je 0x466870` skips only the count on a failed placement and the loop continues. VCMI `BattleInfo.cpp:247` recreates `obidgen` per iteration and its `catch` at `:290` is outside the `while`; engine `terrain.rs:199-215`: `pick(rng, &usual)` with replacement, `break` when `anchors` is empty.
- fix: keep a drawn-set (a bitmask over the usual types of the field) and `continue` instead of `break` when no anchor exists; document the choice in the rule either way.

### F-r1-3: No test pins the Magic Plains raise of the periodic casts, the strike's spells and the opening spells
