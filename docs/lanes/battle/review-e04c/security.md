## Findings

### F-sec-1: The observation tells the attacker (and spectators) whether the defender's town has an Escape Tunnel
- kind: leak
- severity: medium (high by the scale's letter if the user rules the tunnel hidden; the docs call it public today, and the fact is worth little in play)
- confidence: high that it shows (traced); medium that the original hides it: its controls are computed for the acting side only (`docs/formats/installation.md:2849-2856`; batch 3g, `E04c-log.md:341-344`: the hot-seat window shows the acting side's buttons), and a town's buildings are no public fact; an experiment (does any enemy view of a Stronghold show the tunnel?) or the user's ruling would raise it
- evidence: `observe.rs:195` fills `may_retreat` for both sides from `state.may_retreat` (`state.rs:1258-1260`), which at a town is `side == Attacker || s.escape_tunnel` for the defender (`siege.rs:408-412`). It reaches the flat encoding as "opponent may retreat" (`h3-sim/src/observation.rs:366-367`) and the wire's per-side `SideView.may_retreat` (`h3-protocol/src/map/mod.rs:325-329`). The same bit also rides in `Observation.siege` (the whole `SiegeState` is copied, `observe.rs:240`; its private field `escape_tunnel` is in `Debug` output and in `PartialEq`). The choice was taken knowingly ("the controls were public already, and the tunnel shows in them", `docs/epics/E04c-log.md:338-339`; BAT-SIE-024's catalog summary), but neither ADR-0023 nor `06-security.md` weighs it against the original. Scenario: a Stronghold with the tunnel, a defending hero, no Shackles: from the first observation the attacker's `may_retreat` is `[true, true]`; without the tunnel `[true, false]`. A spectator sees the same.
- The same pattern elsewhere in E04c: none found. The other setup facts in the public part are public by ADR-0023 points 5 and 7 and public in effect (fort, moat, walls, gate, `difficulty`, `human`; the town's morale and luck show in the stacks' morale and luck, as the original's creature window does; `commands_catapult` and the defender's `commands_ballista` show anyway through `Observation.active`). One unconfirmed: the turrets' creature counts are `turret_count + structures` (`state.rs:515`), so the observation tells the town's building count (`Siege.structures`); whether the original's tower window shows its strength was not checked (left open).
- fix or test sketch: a user decision first. If hidden: compute the other side's `may_retreat` at a town without the tunnel (or mark it unknown), the spectator's for neither side; keep the tunnel out of the observed `SiegeState` (a separate state field, or zeroed in `observe_as`); update BAT-SIE-024/BAT-FLE-001's "public", the encoding and wire semantics (version notes). Test (needs-run): two setups differing only in `Siege.escape_tunnel` (Stronghold-like record, defending hero), same seed; assert `observe(.., Attacker)` and `observe_as(.., Spectator)` equal, the defender's differing only in its own `may_retreat`; add the tunnel to `observations_do_not_depend_on_hidden_state` the same way (two setups, since the field is private to `siege`). If public: say so in ADR-0023 point 1 and `06-security.md` with the original's difference.

### F-sec-2: The automatic turrets' and Ballista's aim reads the other side's private Armorer and Artillery level, and its choice is public
- kind: side channel
- severity: low
- confidence: high that the reads exist (traced); low that a player can exploit them (it needs the aim's x87 worth model; the original's computer reads the same, so the original shows the same choice)
- evidence: `aim::protection` takes the hit stack's controller's hero reduction (`aim.rs:92-101`, line 100), used by `worth` (194) and `score` (512); `worth` grows a Ballista's offense by its controller's Artillery level (`aim.rs:201-210`, from `hero::ballista`, private per `hero.rs:48` and `OwnHeroView.ballista`); `context` sums `worth` over both sides (`aim.rs:336-339`), so desperate mode reads them too; `ballista_target` reads the target's reduction again through `estimate` → `shot_damage` (`aim.rs:592`, `combat.rs:375-397`). The target is told to both sides (the shot's events are public). Scenario: a Castle siege, the defender's turrets automatic; the attacker commands its Ballista with Artillery 1 or 3; the keep's `turret_target` may pick the Ballista at level 3 (offense x3) and another stack at level 1 (x1.5): the defender learns a bound on the attacker's Artillery. The reduction already shows in every damage number on that side, so only the level is new information. The lane HANDOFF's trap names pits only.
- fix or test sketch: decide and document (06-security's battle list or ADR-0023 point 7: the computer's choices read what the original's computer reads, the opponent's Armorer and Artillery level included), as BAT-Q31 did for the berserk walk; or replace the two reads with public values (not faithful). Test (needs-run), if the channel is to stay closed: a Castle state with the keep next to act, twins differing only in the attacker's `ballista.level` (1 vs 3, `shots` and chance unchanged), apply the same legal command, compare the events projected to the defender.

### F-sec-3: The hygiene properties vary none of E04c's private facts
- kind: test-gap
- severity: low
- confidence: high (read)
- evidence: `observations_do_not_depend_on_hidden_state` (`tests/properties.rs:1514-1621`) changes, per side, the RNG, tactics, damage bonuses, ballista control with `level: 1` fixed (1553), tent, discount, funds, book, masteries, bonuses and hidden magic, and compares observations at one state. It never changes `HeroRecord.catapult` (the Ballistics level, private), never another Artillery level, never the Escape Tunnel (F-sec-1 would fail it), and runs no automatic turn after the change (F-sec-2 would show only there). `tests/observe.rs` has no town. `h3-protocol`'s frame hygiene tests play the `full` scenario only (`h3-protocol/src/tests/session.rs:1747, 1776`; "siege" appears only in `tests/mapping.rs`).
- fix or test sketch: in the twin loop add `hero.catapult = hero.catapult.and(Some(CatapultControl { level: 1 + level % 3 }))` and vary `BallistaControl.level`; add the two-setup tunnel case (F-sec-1); add a town case to `tests/observe.rs` (the defender sees its mines with `revealed: false`, the attacker and a spectator none); the server lane could add a siege scenario to its frame tests.

## Gaps in the hygiene properties

- `observations_do_not_depend_on_hidden_state`: at a town nothing siege-specific is varied (F-sec-3). It compares observations, never the events of the computer's automatic turns after a hidden change.
- `hidden_obstacles_do_not_show` (`properties.rs:1726-1850`) covers the town mines: at a `t:necropolis` town with a Citadel and a moat the twin drops every obstacle the viewer does not see, the mines included, and compares the observation, legal commands, castable spells, cast and second targets (the first of each), creature casts and the projected events of each of the viewer's commands, the turrets' and the Catapult's automatic turns inside `apply` included. Limits: one state per game; only the viewer's own commands; commands that reveal an obstacle are skipped (correct); BAT-QUIRK-4 is off in its profiles.
- The walls property (`walls_and_the_moat_agree_with_movement`, 2105) checks movement against the walls, not hidden state.

## Bounded work and storage (no finding)

- Wall line: at most `17 x 16` steps (`siege.rs:614-640`). Catapult: at most 8 targets, `shots <= MAX_WALL_SHOTS` (compiler, `h3-content-battle/src/siege.rs:251-262`); its automatic aim always finds a standing target while one stands (`siege.rs:796-823`). Earthquake: `hits` (data) x 8 (`siege.rs:927-958`). Aim: per turret or Ballista turn O(stacks x (hexes + stacks)), Dial's queue holds each position once (`aim.rs:365-445`: a position's entry cost is monotone in the cost it is entered from, so the first push is its least).
- Storage: `parts`, `kinds`, `opens`, `turrets` are `[_; MAX_WALL_TARGETS]`, the targets compiler-bounded to 8 (`h3-content-battle/src/siege.rs:262`); turrets take only the defender's free records (`state.rs`, `room` from `SIDE_STACK_SLOTS`), never short with 7 army slots and 4 machines.
- Endless siege: battles have no round limit by design (`properties.rs:2011`). One side cannot force it: the automatic Catapult shoots each round until nothing stands, turrets always shoot a living attacker stack, the attacker may retreat (unless Shackles), the defender can walk out through the gate. Mutual stalling (both Defend) never ends in the engine, at a town as anywhere; the host's turn timers or the sim's `max_steps` (`h3-sim/src/env.rs:76-82`) end it. Within one command automatic turns run until a stack awaits a command (`turn.rs:245-290`); bounded by the effects' durations.
- C ABI: E04c's wall-shot entries and siege observation fields are covered by the existing checks (null `env`/`out`, `len >= ACTION_COUNT`/`OBSERVATION_LEN`, `lib.rs:327-341, 482-492`; entry decode `action.rs:290-292`; panics caught, `lib.rs:197-199`); the 30 globals match `GLOBALS - MAX_WALL_TARGETS` (`observation.rs:130, 358-390`). No finding.
