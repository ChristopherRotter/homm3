## Findings

### F-r2b-1: A frenzied striker standing in the moat keeps its full defense for Frenzy's attack bonus; the original subtracts the moat's 3 there too
- rules: BAT-SIE-006 (and BAT-MAG-019, whose source already cites 0x442590 for the Behemoths' share of the own defense)
- kind: behaviour-bug (and rule-text: BAT-SIE-006's summary names only the struck stack)
- severity: medium (a rare setup: Frenzy on a stack that stands in the moat; up to 6 attack points at Expert Frenzy, up to 30 % damage)
- confidence: high on the code; VCMI agrees. An experiment row would settle it beyond doubt (a frenzied stack with defense d in the moat striking: the original's damage uses attack + (d − 3) × v).
- evidence: `crates/h3-battle/src/combat.rs:349–359` (`frenzy_defense`: `effective_defense(striker)` and the Behemoths' quirk, no moat term) feeding `frenzied(...)` at `combat.rs:250–257`; `crates/h3-battle/src/siege.rs:511–520` is applied only to the victim at `combat.rs:239`. The original: 0x442410 (`stack_attack_against`) at 0x442501–0x442534: while the striker is frenzied (+0x278) it calls 0x442590 on *itself* (ecx = esi, flag 0), multiplies the result by the frenzy factor (+0x498) and adds the attack; 0x442590 at 0x4425F7–0x442646 subtracts 3 when the moat flag is set and either of the stack's hexes is a moat hex (0x469DC0), after the Behemoths' share and without a floor; the flag 0 only skips the "struck → 0" branch at 0x442593–0x4425AB. VCMI: `config/spells/moats.json:69–76` gives units on the moat hexes a PRIMARY_SKILL defence −3 bonus (`lib/spells/effects/Moat.cpp:83–134`, a `UnitOnHexLimiter`) and `lib/battle/CUnitState.cpp:743–751` computes the frenzy bonus from that bonus value. Scenario: a Citadel (any moat kind, the Tower excluded), a stack with defense 10 under Basic Frenzy (v = 100 %) standing in the moat strikes a stack of defense 10 with attack 10 and damage 20: the original's attack is 10 + (10 − 3) = 17 (+35 %: 27), the engine's 10 + 10 = 20 (+50 %: 30).
- fix or test sketch: give `frenzy_defense` the state and subtract `siege::defense_penalty(state, striker)` (not floored; the sum may go negative, the original's `ftol` of a negative f32 truncates toward 0 and `stack_attack_against` returns it as is). Test: the `strike` helper of `the_moat_lowers_the_defense_below_zero_but_not_a_frenzied_stack_s` with `frenzy` on stack 0 (the striker on (8, 4) must stand in the moat: swap the positions) expecting 27 against 30 without a moat; `needs-run`.

### F-r2b-2: The bow's exemption from the walls' halving follows the shooter's own side's hero, the original the controlling side's (a hypnotized shooter)
- rules: BAT-SIE-008 (the same pattern in BAT-RNG-002/BAT-HER-006 for the distance's penalty, outside this slice)
- kind: behaviour-bug
- severity: low (a hypnotized shooter outside the walls shooting at a target inside, with a Golden Bow or Bow of the Sharpshooter on exactly one hero)
- confidence: high on the code reading (0x467510, 0x467520–0x46753A; §7.25 reads +0x288 as hypnotized); whether the engine ever re-derives traits for a hypnotized stack I did not find (`hero::trait_effects` runs once at the setup, `state.rs:740`).
- evidence: `crates/h3-battle/src/hero.rs:214–240` sets `NO_WALL_PENALTY` from `setup.heroes[side]` at `state.rs:740–745`; `combat.rs:281–287` passes `striker.has(NO_WALL_PENALTY)`. The original at 0x46751A–0x46753A takes the side as 1 − +0xF4 while +0x288 is set, and reads that side's hero at +0x53CC for the artifacts 91 and 137 (0x4675AE–0x4675D7).
- fix or test sketch: compute the exemption at the shot from the controller's hero (`hero::damage`-style lookup by `striker.controller()` for a `NoRangePenalty` matching the creature) instead of a setup trait, for the walls and the distance alike; a test with a hypnotized attacker's shooter whose own hero wears the bow.
