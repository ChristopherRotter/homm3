# 03 — Domain model: bounded contexts and language

This is the first cut. Contexts are refined when their epics start. Splitting or merging is allowed but must be recorded (ADR) because it changes crate boundaries.

## Context map

| Context | Responsibility | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| **Kernel** (shared kernel) | IDs, numerics, PRNG, hashing, errors, modifier primitives (ADR-0012) | — |
| **Content** | Static definitions: creature types, spells, artifacts, skills, hero classes/heroes, town types/buildings, terrain, object types; content packs; `Ruleset` compilation | Kernel |
| **Contracts** | Seam types exchanged between contexts (`BattleSetup`, `BattleOutcome`, …) | Kernel, Content |
| **Battle** | Tactical combat: hex field, stacks, turn order (initiative), movement, attacks, retaliation, morale/luck, damage, obstacles, war machines, battle spells, surrender/retreat, outcome | Kernel, Content, Contracts |
| **Adventure** | Map, tiles/terrain, heroes on the map and their armies, objects and their behaviors, movement and pathing, fog of war/exploration, resources on map, visiting, guards/monsters, events (`h3-adventure`, ADR-0016) | Kernel, Content, Contracts, the hero model |
| **Town** | Buildings and construction, weekly growth, recruitment, town-specific mechanics (mage guild, market, etc.) (`h3-towns`, ADR-0028: a town's record, the store of towns, the town part; the town on the map is the Adventure's) | Kernel, Content, Contracts, the hero model |
| **Heroes & Army** | The hero model (`h3-heroes`, shared layer beneath the contexts, ADR-0013): hero details, their validation, what a hero has from them in every context, hero changes; hero progression (levels, primary/secondary skills, specialties), artifacts and equipment, spell book, army composition/garrison | Kernel, Content, Contracts |
| **Magic** | Spell definitions' effects as used by battle and adventure (may live inside Battle/Adventure initially) | Kernel, Content |
| **Game** (application) | The whole game's setup and state: players, teams, turn groups and the calendar (day/week/month), RNG streams per player, pending decisions, victory/defeat conditions, diplomacy/alliances, scenario setup, command routing, visibility/projection (`h3-game`, ADR-0017) | the domain contexts it wires |

Outside the domain: **Formats/Import** (anti-corruption layer for original formats and archives), **Sim/AI**, **FFI**, **CLI**, **Server**, **Clients**.

## Seams (the important one: adventure ↔ battle)

Adventure and Battle never import each other.

1. Adventure/Game detect an engagement and build a `BattleSetup`: armies, heroes' *derived battle profile* (attack/defense/power/knowledge, relevant skills, artifacts' effects, spell book and mana), terrain/battlefield type, obstacles, siege info, rules-relevant flags. All derived values are computed **before** the seam, so Battle needs no knowledge of hero management.
2. Battle runs standalone from a `BattleSetup` (this is also what lets the battle AI train in isolation).
3. Battle returns a `BattleOutcome`: result (a victory of one side, or a draw when both armies are wiped out at once), casualties, survivors, experience inputs, artifacts/spell-scroll capture, etc. Game applies it to adventure/hero state.

Other seams (town ↔ heroes, heroes ↔ magic, content ↔ all) are introduced the same way: a small contract type, owned by `contracts`, no reach-through.

## Ubiquitous language (glossary — extend as contexts grow)

- **Ruleset** — immutable compiled content + parameters + enabled rule modules for a game.
- **Content pack** — a set of definitions (`base`, `ab`, `sod`, later others) with a manifest, namespaced IDs and load order.
- **Profile** — a named set of quirk toggles/parameters (e.g., `faithful`, `strict-fixed`).
- **Quirk** — a known deviation/bug of the original, classified in the quirks ledger.
- **Stack** — a number of identical creatures acting as one unit (in an army or in battle).
- **Army** — up to N stacks (N is a ruleset parameter, 7 in the original) belonging to a hero or garrison.
- **Hero**, **Town**, **Dwelling**, **Garrison**, **Mine**, **Resource**, **Artifact**, **Spell**, **Skill (primary/secondary)**.
- **Hero** — the person across contexts; never a type name (ADR-0013). **Hero type** (`HeroType`, content) — the definition: class, specialty, starting skills and army. **Hero details** (`HeroDetails`, the hero model `h3-heroes`) — a hero's lasting state: level, experience, primary and secondary skills, artifacts, spell book, mana; not the army. **Store of heroes** — the game's one record of details per hero (by `HeroId`, as the original's); the contexts read it by reference and change it by **hero changes** (`HeroChange`: typed, checked by the hero model), which the game applies (ADR-0013 points 1–2, ADR-0017 point 1). **Battle hero** (`BattleHero`, contract) — what a battle needs, all derived from the details before the seam. The **hero on the map** is an Adventure type (E08): its tile, army and the army's formation, its **tactics flag** (off: the tactics phase it would get is denied; E08 increment 6), movement points. **Battle bonuses** — a morale and a luck bonus in the hero details, added by rewards and cleared when the hero's next battle ends, with the **once-marks** of the bonuses a hero takes once (the Warrior's Tomb's, the Pyramid's; ADR-0013 points 1, 15.1).
- **Specialty** — a hero type's built-in bonus (skill, creature, resource, spell, fixed creature stats, speed, creature upgrade, dragons). **Effect** — a typed primitive of an artifact, a skill level or a specialty (`HeroEffect` in content); the translation turns effects into resolved **battle effects** (`BattleEffect`).
- **Artifact type** (`ArtifactType`, content) — the definition of an artifact: class, value, slot, effects, a war machine it places, the parts of a **combination artifact**. **Artifact slot** (`ArtifactSlot`) — a kind of equipment position with a count (two rings, four or five misc positions); an artifact fits any position of its slot, an artifact without a slot is carried in the backpack only. A combination occupies its own slot and **locks** one position of each other part's slot. Which artifacts a hero wears belongs to the hero details: **worn** artifacts (by slot kind, positions interchangeable; by **position** in the original's order from E08 T49, ADR-0013 point 1) and the **backpack** (up to 64 artifacts; never war machines or the spell book), HER-ART-001/002. **Hero parameters** (`HeroParams`, Tier 2) — the limits of hero details (primary skills, backpack; the **mana ceiling** a reward holds a hero's spell points under, 999 in the original).
- **Tactics phase** — the placement phase before round 1 for the side with the higher Tactics level. **Retreat** — a side with a hero leaves the battle and loses its army. **Surrender** — a side with a hero buys its way out of a battle against another hero, keeping its army.
- **Adventure map**, **Tile**, **Object**, **Fog of war**. **Static map** — what never changes after setup (terrain, roads, rivers, the objects' fixed data), shared by every fork (ADR-0016). **Object kind** — the closed behaviour of a map object (`Blocker` first), with typed parameters from data; an **object type** maps the original's classes to a kind. An object type names its visit properties as data (ADR-0016 point 4): **entered** (a step onto its visitable tile visits it and ends the move) or **visited from a neighbouring tile** (the hero stays where it stands), and **visitable from above** or not. A **blocked tile** cannot be entered; a **visitable tile** is visited by entering it or from a neighbouring tile, by its type. **Occupancy** — which tiles the objects still on the map block or make visitable, a persistent layer cleared when an object leaves the map. **Explored** — a tile a player has seen; it stays explored, showing its current content, until a rule hides it again (Cover of Darkness). **Fringe** — the hidden tiles touching an explored tile: their terrain, roads and rivers show (ADR-0016 point 5). **Wandering monster** — a monster object on the map (a creature and count, a disposition): it **guards** the tiles around it (a step onto a **guarded tile** ends the move and visits the guard) and answers a visit with a battle or an offer (join, join for gold, pursue a fleeing monster); its **split** divides its count into stacks for the battle. **Guarded object** — an object whose guards must be fought before it is visited (a guarded pile; ADR-0016 point 4). **Reward record** — a Pandora's Box's or a map event's static list of what it gives (experience, primary and secondary skills, mana, morale and luck, resources, artifacts, spells, creatures), given in the original's order (ADR-0013 point 15.2, ADR-0016 point 4). **Map event** (an **event object**; not a game event) — a hidden object holding a reward record and its colours: a hero of such a colour that steps onto its tile fires it and its move ends there; it is never observed and the reachable-tiles query never reads it (ADR-0016 points 4, 6). **Once-per-hero mark** — the adventure's record, by hero id, of which objects of a once-per-hero class a hero has visited (ADR-0016 point 1). **Knowledge** (of an object) — what a player learned of an object by a visit of its heroes (a Shrine's spell, a hut's skill, a find emptied), shown in its observation from then on (ADR-0016 point 6). **Boat** — a vessel on a water tile, no map object because it moves: a record (its type, its facing, its owner and last captain) and, while empty, a tile of the empty boats' layer (ADR-0016 point 1); any hero boards any empty boat. **In a boat** — a hero on the map that holds a boat: it moves over water only, at the sea's prices, with its day's sea points, and neither flies nor walks on water (ADR-0016 points 3, 4; ADR-0013 point 13.6). **Boarding** — a step from land onto an empty boat's tile; **landing** — a step from a boat onto a free coastal tile; both end the move and switch the hero's **day's maximum** to the new surface's, its points to 0 or, with **free boarding** (the Admiral's Hat), in proportion (ADR-0016 point 3). **Free coastal tile** — a land tile the map flags as coast with no object blocking it or visited from it, no event, no hole and no hero: the only tile a hero in a boat lands on (ADR-0016 point 4). **Ground of the sea** — an object lying on water that is never visited and changes a boat's step price (Favorable Winds: two thirds), as a special ground changes a walker's on land (ADR-0016 point 4). **Ownable object** — an object a player can own (a mine, an abandoned mine, a Lighthouse, a Shipyard, a town from E09; dwellings from E08 increment 10); its **owner** is a player or none (**neutral**), public to every viewer that sees the object (ADR-0016 points 1, 6). **Flag** — a visit's change of an ownable object's owner to its visitor's owner (from a neutral or another team's owner, never an own or allied one), revealing radius 3 around its entrance (ADR-0016 points 4, 5). **Mine** — an ownable object producing a resource a day for its owner; an **abandoned mine** draws its resource and its Troglodytes when the game is built (ADR-0016 point 4). **Guards** (of a mine) — the army on the mine: fought by another player's hero after a question, played by the mine's owner or the neutral seat; their survivors stay (ADR-0017 point 11). **Leave Guards** — the army dialog an owner's hero opens at its own mine, whose group is kept on the mine as its guards when it closes (ADR-0016 point 3, ADR-0017 point 6). **Weekly object** — an object owned by nobody whose contents (the Windmill's resource, the Water Wheel's gold, the Mystical Garden's gift) the week's first visit by any player's hero takes, empty for everyone until the next week; a visit also marks the visitor's team, a hint only (ADR-0016 point 4). **Dwelling** (on the adventure map; a town's dwellings are E09's) — an ownable object (classes 17 and 20) holding one to four creatures and their counts, each set to its growth when the game is built and on every week's first day, never added; its **guards** are its own: for each creature of level 5 or more three times its growth, renewed every week whoever owns it, fought unsplit and unowned by another team's visitor after a question, never its owner's troops (no Leave Guards); won or unguarded, it is flagged (ADR-0016 point 4, ADR-0017 point 11). **Random dwelling** — a dwelling whose faction set and level range the map leaves open, drawn when the game is built into the dwelling of that faction and level, with that dwelling's own footprint and its anchor moved as the original moves it (ADR-0016 points 1, 4). **Refugee Camp** — an object owned by nobody and unguarded, whose creature (a random monster of levels 1 to 7) and count (its growth) are drawn when the game is built and every week, hired by any player's hero (ADR-0016 point 4). **Free creatures** — an own dwelling's level-1 creatures, which join its visitor's army free, all of each, or none while the army has no room (ADR-0016 point 3). **Hire question** — the offer to recruit a dwelling's or a camp's creatures; accepted, the free creatures join and, for any creature above level 1, the **hire dialog** opens: its owner's pending decision to buy creatures, each at most its count and what the treasury and the army's room allow, open until closed (a one-creature dialog closes after its purchase), read-only at an ally's dwelling (ADR-0017 point 6, GAM-DEC-008). **Teleporter** — an entered object that moves its visitor to another tile of the map: a monolith, a Subterranean Gate or a Whirlpool (ADR-0016 point 4). **Channel** — the teleporters a jump links, built with the game into the static map without a draw: a one-way channel's exits, a two-way channel's members, every Whirlpool, a gate's pair (ADR-0016 point 1). **Entrance** — the teleporter a hero steps onto; **exit** — the tile a jump moves it to: drawn among the channel's candidates (one uniform draw from the visitor's stream for two or more), a gate's pair (ADR-0016 point 4, ADR-0017 point 4). **Jump** — the entrance's visit: the hero moves to the exit without a step, keeping its points and the day's maximum, with its boat if in one; it fights no guard and visits nothing there, an enemy hero on the exit is fought first, and no path goes through a teleporter (ADR-0016 points 3, 4). **Monolith** — a teleporter of a numbered channel: a one-way entrance, a one-way exit (it leads nowhere) or a two-way monolith (to another of its channel) (ADR-0016 point 4). **Subterranean Gate** — a teleporter paired when the game is built with the nearest gate of another level; one without a pair leads nowhere; a hero of the mover's team on the pair is met and the mover stays (ADR-0016 point 4). **Whirlpool** — a teleporter on water, reached by boat, whose jump goes to another Whirlpool's tile after **a Whirlpool's loss**: the visitor's weakest stack (the least fight value × count) keeps half its count, rounded down, a lone stack at least one; the Sea Captain's Hat spares it (`whirlpool_protection`) (ADR-0016 point 4). **Revisit** — a visit again of the entered object under a hero, without a step or points (the original's space key; `Revisit`, ADR-0017 point 1).
- **Day / Week / Month** — the calendar (weekly growth, month/week events). **Day's start** — on every day advance from day 2, in the original's order: the calendar, the **week's step** on a week's first day (days 8, 15, …: the weekly objects' new contents, then every wandering monster's growth in sixteenths), the **month's kind** and the **month's step** on a month's first day (days 29, 57, …: normal, a creature's month whose monsters appear on free tiles, or the Plague), the **income**, then every hero's spell points (ADR-0017 point 3, ADR-0016 point 4). **Income** — what a player receives at the day's start: its mines' production, its heroes' artifacts, skills and specialties (ADR-0013 point 16.1) and on a week's first day its armies' `weekly_resource` creatures (the Crystal Dragons' crystal), paid to its treasury (ADR-0017 point 10). **Timed event** — a map's scheduled gift or loss of resources to its players, fired at a player's turn start on its days, a loss floored at 0 (ADR-0017 point 3). **Turn group** — the players who act together; a day is an ordered list of turn groups, one player each in sequential play (ADR-0017). **Pending decision** — a question a player must answer (a level-up, an object's question, a battle), held in the state instead of a blocking prompt; while it is open the player's only legal commands are its answers. **Offer** — a pending decision answered yes or no, with an optional price (the Cartographer's, the border guard's; ADR-0017 point 6). A **meeting** (two of a player's heroes, opened by a step toward the other) and an **army dialog** (a hero and a joining group that found no room) are its owner's pending decisions in which it **manages** armies and artifacts, closed by `Decline` (ADR-0017 point 6, E08 increment 6). A **battle** on the map is a pending decision owned by the players of its sides; a side no player owns (monsters, guards) is an **unowned side**, played by the system actor (ADR-0017 points 2 and 11). **Treasury** — a player's resources, one amount per resource kind, held by the game (ADR-0017 point 1). **Setup** — plain data a game, or a context, is built and validated from (`GameSetup`, `AdventureSetup`); a map is imported into one (ADR-0015). **Ruleset part** — a context's compiled rules beside the core ruleset (ADR-0006 amendment).
- **Town** — split between two contexts, each fact held once (ADR-0028 point 2): **a town's record** (`h3-towns`: its faction, the buildings **built** and **forbidden**, its events, its name), kept in the **store of towns** (the game's, by **`TownId`**: the town's index among the map's towns in object order, a contract id), and its map object of kind `Town` (the Adventure's: tiles and entrance, owner, garrison, visiting hero; ADR-0016 points 1, 3, 4). **Town record** — a faction's entry in the **town part** (`TownRuleset`, `packs/*/towns.toml`): its buildings, the silo's resources, the moat and the siege's natural obstacles (ADR-0028 point 5). **Garrison** — a town's army on the map, held by the Adventure as a mine's guards. **Visiting hero** — the hero standing on a town's entrance, never a stored field. **Capture** — a town's change of owner: entering an undefended town of another team or none, or after a won siege by a later step (the attacker stays on the tile it attacked from); the garrison emptied, the reveal by the town's radius, the Capitol's limit; a town is never flagged (ADR-0016 point 4, ADR-0017 point 10). **Siege's facts** — `Siege` (faction, fort level, moat, structures, Escape Tunnel, the buildings' morale and luck bonuses), derived by `h3-towns` from a town and its faction's town record for every battle at a town (ADR-0023 point 2). **Hall** — a town's Village Hall, Town Hall, City Hall or Capitol; the highest built gives the day's gold. **Capitol** — the highest hall, one per player: a captured town's Capitol whose new owner holds another becomes a City Hall. **Fort level** — none, Fort, Citadel or Castle, the highest built (walls and gate, the keep and moat, the towers in a siege). **Hero pool** — the game's record per hero type of whether it is free, taken or out of the game and which colours may take it; starting heroes are drawn from it (ADR-0017 point 1). **Faction choice** — a player's faction or Random, chosen before the game is built within its allowed factions. **Starting-hero pick** — a hero type from the original's offer (the first 16 free heroes of the chosen faction's two classes) or none, reserved before any draw. **Main town** — a player's town the map names, which may generate the player's starting hero as its visitor (ADR-0017 points 1, 4). **Build** — a town's owner, in its turn, buying one building of the town's faction from the treasury: the **town command** `TownCommand::Build` (`h3-towns`' type, wrapped as `GameCommand::Town`), no hero needed in the town (ADR-0028 point 6, ADR-0017 point 1). **Hall's offer** — the buildings a town can build now: not ever built, every requirement in the ever-built set, not forbidden, no Grail building, a Capitol only while its player holds none, a Shipyard only with water, the day's build not yet made; a horde whose level's upgraded dwelling stands is offered as its upgraded horde (ADR-0028 point 2). **Day's build** (the **day's mark**) — whether a building was bought in a town today: one a town a day for every player, cleared at every day's start, kept at a capture unless the town passes from a team without a human to one with one; public where the town is seen (ADR-0028 point 2, ADR-0017 point 3). **Requirements** and **cost** — a building's direct requirements (buildings of its town record, closed by the compiler) and its resources, the town part's (ADR-0028 point 5). **Boat spot** — the water tile below a town's entrance, fixed when the game is built (the Adventure's); without one the town's Shipyard is forbidden (ADR-0016 point 1, ADR-0017 point 4). **Town event** — a map's event of one town (not a timed event, not a map event object): on its days, at its owner's turn start after the timed events, it gives resources, builds buildings without the hall's tests (only what stands, what the faction lacks, what its own buildings supersede and the Shipyard's water count) and adds creatures (from increment 3) (ADR-0017 point 3). **Available creatures** — a town's creatures to recruit, a count by its faction's **index** (the levels' seven creatures, then their upgrades; an upgraded dwelling's count serves its upgrade and its base creature), grown each week by every standing dwelling's **growth** (the creature's, the castle's, the Statue of Legion's share, the Legion's pieces on the garrison hero and the visitor, a horde, the owner's flagged dwellings, the Grail) and kept in the town's record (ADR-0028 point 2). **Week's kind** — normal, a **creature's week** (+5 growth to one creature) or a `week_of` building's week (the Deity of Fire's Imps), drawn at each week's first day from the **towns' stream** and public, as the **month's kind** is (ADR-0017 points 3, 4). **Portal of Summoning** — the Dungeon's building that each week summons one creature of one of its owner's flagged dwellings, its count that creature's growth, set and not added (ADR-0028 point 2). **Garrison hero** — a hero of the town's owner moved into the town's garrison: off the map, its whole record kept, defending a siege with its own army (ADR-0016 point 3, ADR-0023 point 3). **Upper bar** — the town screen's army row: the garrison hero's army when one stands, else the town's garrison, where recruits go; the **lower bar** is the visitor's (ADR-0016 point 3, ADR-0017 point 6).
- **Battlefield**, **Hex**, **Initiative/Speed**, **Retaliation**, **Morale**, **Luck**, **Obstacle**.
- **War machine** — a machine a side brings into battle besides its army (Ballista, First Aid Tent, Ammo Cart; later siege machines): a stack with flag `war_machine` at a fixed hex, controlled automatically without the hero's skill, never retaliating, unaffected by morale; a side with only war machines left has lost.
- **Round** — every living stack acts once (normal phase, fastest first; then the **wait phase**, slowest first). **Active stack** — the stack whose turn it is. **Wait** — postpone the turn to the wait phase (once per round). **Defend** — end the turn with a defense bonus until the stack's next turn. **Attack** — one swing of a stack (its melee attack, a second attack, a retaliation or a shot); it strikes the target and the secondary targets its pattern hits (breath, multi-headed, area shots). **Strike** — the damage application of an attack on one stack. **Formation** — how an army is arranged at the start of a battle: **loose** (spread from top to bottom) or **tight** (clustered around the middle), a per-army choice; the ruleset gives the starting rows by stack count for each.
- **Ability** — a typed behaviour primitive of a creature type (flying, hate, jousting, …), referenced from content (ADR-0012). **Modifier** — a numeric change to a stat of a bearer (stack, side) with a source and an expiry; effective stats are materialised from base stats and modifiers (ADR-0012).
- **Spell school** — air, earth, fire or water; a spell belongs to one or more. **Mastery** — the level (none, Basic, Advanced, Expert) at which a hero casts a spell: the best of its schools' masteries (the magic school skills), raised by an artifact (Armageddon's Blade), derived once per spell in the Heroes context (E04b T8a). **Learned spells** — what a hero keeps whatever it wears; its **available spells** add the spells of its worn artifacts and **scrolls** (an **artifact instance** holding a spell); both the adventure map and the battle use the one derivation in the Heroes context. **Spell book** — what lets a hero cast its available spells (the Spell Book artifact). **Battle magic** (`SpellMagic`, content) — what a spell does in battle: the **target** its caster names (none, a stack, a hex), the stacks a **cast** reaches (an **area** around a hex, a **chain** from a stack, or every stack meeting its **target conditions**) and its **spell effects** (closed kinds: damage, timed, … ; ADR-0014). A **mass version** — from a mastery (usually Expert) the spell takes no target and reaches every stack of its side. **Timed effect** — what a timed spell leaves on a stack for a number of rounds (or the rest of the battle): an entry of the battle's **effect list** (one per stack and spell) with the changes made at the cast, given back when it **ends**; **counter** spells end each other's effects (Bless and Curse).
- **Side** (`BattleSide`) — attacker or defender in a battle; the battle context's actor identity. A stack's side is its owner; its **controller** is the side that commands its turns and whose hero's bonuses it fights with, the other side while it is **hypnotized** (BAT-MAG-026). A **berserk** stack takes automatic turns against the nearest stack and is every stack's **enemy** (BAT-MAG-025); otherwise stacks of different controllers are enemies.
- Sim-layer terms (not domain language): **episode**, **step**, **truncation** (step limit), **action index**, **observation encoding**, **reward**.
- **Command** — a player's intent, validated and applied. **Event** — a fact emitted by applying a command. **Observation** — a player-specific projection of state. **Actor** — the trusted identity issuing a command.
- **Rule** — a specified piece of game logic with an ID in the rule catalog.

Use these words in code, docs, tests and commit messages. If the original game's term and a technical term differ, the original game's term wins inside domain code.
